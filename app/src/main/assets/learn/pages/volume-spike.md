# Warning: a volume spike

> **In short:** A day with far more trading than usual can mean news or a sharp move. It is a warning only.

**This is a warning, never a paper trade.**

## What it looks for

A day whose volume is more than **{{VOLUME_SPIKE_MULTIPLE}} times** its average over the previous {{VOLUME_SPIKE_DAYS}} days. It is read from the daily chart.

## Why it is a warning and not a signal

Heavy volume feels like interest, but in crypto the research points the other way: high abnormal volume on Binance predicted **lower** returns the next day, in coins that are hard to bet against (Garfinkel, Hsiao and Hu, 2025). Volume also predicts big moves in **both** directions. And exchange-reported volume can be inflated by wash trading, which is trading to fake activity.

So a volume spike means "attention is rising", not "up next".

## What Signal Lab does

It sends a warning and shows it in the coin's Details. It opens no paper trade.
