# Hikkake: a false break that reverses

**Evidence: some proof.** From the same 400-coin study as the Harami.

## What it looks for

1. An **inside candle**: its high is lower and its low is higher than the candle before it.
2. The next candle **breaks down** out of it (lower high and lower low): a false break.
3. The signal fires on that breaking candle. It fires again on the first candle within three after it that closes back **above the high of the inside candle**, which confirms the false break.

## Where it runs

Every chart size. The published test used 1-hour charts.

## How a paper trade ends

Learned: a target and time limit from what this pattern did before (see *What a paper trade is*).

## What the research says

- The same study as the Harami: 400 coins, 36 exchanges, 2018 to 2022, still showing an effect after a data-snooping test (IREF, 2026). Written to match TA-Lib's version candle for candle.

## Honest limits

- The size of the effect after fees is not stated.
- Only the bullish version is used; a false break up is not a signal.
- Rows on charts other than the 1-hour have no outside evidence.
