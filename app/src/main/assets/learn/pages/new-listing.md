# Warning: a new coin

> **In short:** A coin listed only recently has very little history, so anything about it is less reliable.

**This is a warning, never a paper trade.**

## What it means

A coin that Binance listed less than **{{NEW_COIN_DAYS}} days** ago counts as new. Signal Lab adds a line saying so to any alert about it.

## Why it is a warning

A study of 470 Binance listings from 2021 to 2026, dead coins included, found the typical new coin **fell** after listing: median about -4.5% after a day, -11.5% after a week and -21.5% after a month. The rise tended to come **before** the announcement, in the three hours ahead of it (+9.4% on average). It is an unrefereed study, so treat the numbers as a rough guide.

## What Signal Lab does

An alert about a pattern, a pump or a volume spike on a new coin carries that extra line. It does not stop paper trades on it: they are recorded like any other so the scorecard stays honest.

> Research, not financial advice. No real money is ever traded.
