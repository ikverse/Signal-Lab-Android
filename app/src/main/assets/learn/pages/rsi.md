# RSI

> **In short:** A score from 0 to 100 for how hard the price has moved lately. Under 30 means it fell hard. Over 70 means it rose hard.

RSI stands for Relative Strength Index. It compares the size of recent rises with the size of recent falls over a number of candles, usually 14. A coin that has only gone up scores near 100. A coin that has only gone down scores near 0.

## How it is used in the lab

- **RSI crosses above 30.** It was under 30 (it had fallen hard) and has just come back above. People use this to look for a bounce after a fall.
- **RSI is above 70.** The coin has risen hard. People use this to look for strength, or for being stretched, depending on the idea.

The number of candles changes how fast it reacts. A short length jumps around more. A long length is smoother.

## A caution

A low RSI says a coin fell. It does not say it will bounce. Whether an idea built on it works is exactly what a live test is for.
