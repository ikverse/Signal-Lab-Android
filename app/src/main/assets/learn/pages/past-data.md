# Why past results do not decide

> **In short:** An idea made after looking at the data will almost always look good on that same data. Only live practice trades, taken after the idea started, can show whether it works.

When you try an idea on past data, the app replays the history it has stored and shows what the idea would have done. That is useful for catching ideas that never match, or that match so rarely they could never matter. It cannot show that the idea works, because the idea was chosen knowing how that history went.

## What to look at

- **Trades.** A handful of trades says almost nothing. Dozens is better, and the more the better.
- **Won and average.** A win rate close to half with an average near zero means the idea is doing about what chance would do.
- **Every chart size.** An idea that only looks good on one chart size is more likely to be luck.

## What decides

A live test. From the moment you start it, every trade is made without knowing what comes next. After about 30 finished trades the app gives a verdict, and it asks for more evidence the more ideas have been tested. See [How we tell if it is working](learn:scorecard).

> Research, not financial advice. No real money is ever traded.
