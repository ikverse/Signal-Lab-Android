# Moving averages

> **In short:** The average price of the last N candles. A price above its long average usually means the coin has been rising for a while.

A moving average smooths out the ups and downs by averaging the recent closing prices. It moves a little behind the price, which is the point: it shows the direction without the noise.

## Two kinds

- **Average price (SMA).** Every candle counts the same.
- **Fast average (EMA).** Recent candles count more, so it reacts sooner.

## The length matters

A short length, like 20, follows the price closely. A long length, like 200, shows the big picture. A common question is whether the price is above its 200-candle average, which people read as the coin being in a long rise.

## How it is used in the lab

"The price is above the 200-candle average" is a condition that can sit next to others, to make sure an idea only matches while the coin is in a rise. "The price crosses above the 50-candle average" matches only on the candle where the price moves from under to over.
