# Bullish Harami: a small candle inside a big red one

**Evidence: some proof.** Tested on 400 coins; the size of the effect after fees is not stated.

## What it looks for

A **long red candle** (its body bigger than the average of the ten candles before it), followed by a **short candle** (a body no bigger than the average of the ten before it) whose body sits inside the body of the red one, or touches its edge. The signal fires when that second candle closes.

The idea: the fall lost its force.

## Where it runs

Every chart size. The published test used 1-hour charts.

## How a paper trade ends

Learned: a target and time limit from what this pattern did before (see *What a paper trade is*).

## What the research says

- 400 coins across 36 exchanges, 2018 to 2022, found Bullish Harami and Hikkake still showing an effect after a data-snooping test (IREF, 2026). The signal here is written to match TA-Lib, the library that study used, candle for candle.

## Honest limits

- The study did not state how large the effect was after fees.
- Candlestick patterns in other markets are mostly weak; this one's support is one study.
- Signal Lab also watches it on charts other than the 1-hour one the study used. Those rows have no outside evidence at all.
