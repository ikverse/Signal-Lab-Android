# Volume compared with normal

> **In short:** How much trading there is compared with usual. A value of 2 means twice the normal amount.

Volume is how much of a coin traded during a candle. The number alone is hard to read, because a busy coin always has more than a quiet one. So the lab compares each candle's volume with the average volume of the candles before it. The result is a ratio: 1 is normal, 2 is double, 0.5 is half.

## How it is used in the lab

- **Volume compared with normal is above 2.** An unusual burst of trading. It can come with news or a sharp move.
- **Volume compared with normal is below 0.5.** A very quiet stretch.

It is often paired with a price condition, for example the price crossing above its recent high on high volume, because a move on heavy trading is read as more serious than one on thin trading.

> Research, not financial advice. No real money is ever traded.
