# Event-driven and crypto-native strategies: evidence after costs (as of 2026-10-22)

Scope note for the report writer. Fee frame used throughout: 0.25% round trip BTC/ETH, 0.40% other top-50 Binance coins, 0.75% below rank 50. The app is long-only Binance spot, acting on daily, 4-hour and hourly closes with entry at the next candle's open. Where a finding needs a short, perpetual futures, another exchange or minute-level speed, it is flagged as an extension. Vendor marketing and single-source claims are flagged inline. Web search budget ran out near the end; the gaps sections say what could not be verified. Several primary sources (Keyrock, Tokenomist, SSRN, CryptoRank, Coinbase, CoinMarketCal) blocked automated fetches, so some numbers are carried via secondary coverage and are marked as such.

## Key question 1: The Binance spot listing effect (run-up, listing day, the dump; Alpha, futures-first, Seed Tag; the "Upbit effect" and Coinbase listings of Binance-listed coins)

### Takeaway
Across 2019–2026 the pattern is stable: the gain is front-loaded into the days before and the day of listing, the post-listing drift is flat to negative, and it mean-reverts within roughly two weeks. For a long-only app that enters at the next candle's open, a Binance listing is a veto or a fade, not a buy; the only positive-drift pocket found (tokens already on 3+ major venues, median about +7% BTC-adjusted by day 9) rests on 14 observations. The Upbit effect on a Binance-listed coin is real but lives in the first minutes; the only event study with Upbit numbers finds day +1 and days +1 to +3 negative.

### Cited Findings

**Core event studies and large-sample analyses**
- 327 listings of 180 coins on 22 exchanges (to Sept 2019, event study with a BTC market model): average abnormal return +5.7% on listing day (t=0), CAAR +9.2% over (-3,+3), significant at 1%; 59% of events positive over (-3,+3). Pre-event drift (-3,-1) is +5.0%; post-event (+1,+3) is -1.5% and (+1,+2) is -1.2% (both negative, significant on z-test); day +2 is -1.1% with 42% positive. The CAAR peaks at 14.7% on the event day in the (-7,+7) window and the largest share of the gain is built before the event — [Ante 2019, Blockchain Research Lab WP 3](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Exploring-Market-Reactions-to-Exchange-Listings-of-Cryptocurrencies-BRL-working-paper3.pdf)
- Same paper, per exchange: Binance (45 events) +14.7% on t=0 with 76% positive, then (+1,+3) -6.3% with only 24% positive (significant at 5%/1%). Upbit (15 events): day +1 CAAR -2.9% (p<.05) with only 20% positive, (+1,+3) -2.4% (10% level), and the pre-window (-3,-2) -3% (Upbit was the only exchange with a negative pre-window effect). Bithumb +5.1% t=0 (61% positive) then (+1,+3) -6.1%. Coinbase appears with only 7 events and no significant effect — [Ante 2019](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Exploring-Market-Reactions-to-Exchange-Listings-of-Cryptocurrencies-BRL-working-paper3.pdf). Pre-2020 sample; see decay evidence below.
- 1,844 listing events Jan 2023–Jun 2026 on Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Bybit, Kraken, Upbit, Bithumb; BTC-adjusted cumulative VWAP returns, winsorized 1/99: returns rise before Day 0, go flat or negative shortly after, and "largely mean-revert within about two weeks." No exchange delivers a consistently superior BTC-adjusted outcome (differences not statistically meaningful). First major listings show the steepest post-listing decline; tokens with 3+ prior listings hold up best. Smaller market-cap quintiles have the largest initial reaction and the deeper decline, and small-cap underperformance widens at 2–3 months. Tokens with little pre-listing volume spike on other venues have higher D+7 and D+14 returns ("large pre-listing spikes pull demand forward"). Listing date is defined as first trade, so pre-announcement run-ups are only partly captured. Volume: 2023 was the high-water mark; 2024–2025 and 2026 bursts are smaller and fade faster. Single data source (CCData); chart values not in text — [The Tie, "What Does an Exchange Listing Actually Deliver in 2026?"](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026)
- Subset of that work: 14 tokens already live on 3+ major exchanges before a Binance listing. Pre-listing run-up "more muted"; median BTC-adjusted return drifts up after Day 0 to roughly +7% around Day 9, fluctuates near flat to Day 21, then declines (less severe than the full cohort). Cross-exchange 1% depth roughly doubles and stays elevated; spreads compress persistently. n=14, so directional only — [The Tie, Binance listing for already-listed tokens](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-a-binance-listing-delivers-for-already-listed-tokens)
- Binance listings 2017–2024 (500+ listings; 44 instruments in 2024; IQR outliers removed; returns measured from first-day close because day-one returns vs the open average 1,691% and are meaningless): 2024 cohort average +2.78% next day, +0.40% at week 1, -1.76% month 1, -22.66% month 3, -37.64% month 6; only 5.5% of 2024 listings positive at six months. Versus ETH, the 2024 cohort's six-month excess return was -31.20% and only one token beat the market; all-listings six-month average excess return -39.46%. The article also states a first-week average drop of 6.34% (all listings), which conflicts with the 2024 cohort's +0.40% — [Empirica, "The Binance Effect: A 7-Year Analysis"](https://empirica.io/blog/the-binance-effect-a-7-year-analysis-for-token-founders/)
- 2024 listings on Binance, Bybit and MEXC (market-cap ROI indexed to day -1, CoinGecko prices): Binance shows a bullish 1–2 day pop fading by day 7, a small pre-listing rise, then a sell-off to roughly zero ROI by day 30; staggered difference-in-differences finds a significant bullish effect at 7 days for Binance and Bybit "with very wide confidence intervals," and 30/60-day estimates were statistically insignificant. Bybit shows a pre-listing run-up the authors attribute to announcements or insider trading; MEXC drifts down. The authors warn all listing effects appear short-lived and may not hold in bear markets — [RockawayX, "Bullish or Bearish: What Really Happens When Tokens List on a CEX"](https://rockawayx.com/insights/bullish-or-bearish-what-really-happens-when-tokens-list-on-a-cex)
- 389 tokens listed in 2024 on Binance, Bybit, OKX, Coinbase, Bithumb, Upbit; prices snapshot Feb 2–4 2025: average "surge upon listing" 54% overall, Binance 87%, Bybit 61%, Coinbase 41%; share that later "dumped" 89% overall, Binance 98%; average decline Binance -70% from listing price, Bybit -63%, Coinbase -28%. Only three reference points (listing price, ATH, Feb-2025 price), no fixed windows; data-vendor study — [CryptoNinjas/Storible](https://www.cryptoninjas.net/exchange/study-cex-listing-effects/); coverage at [BitPinas](https://bitpinas.com/cryptocurrency/binance-token-listing-dump/)
- Older "Binance effect": 26 coins over 18 months, +41% a day after listing and +24% by day three (Ren & Heinrich; small sample, pre-2024) — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/binance-effect-means-41-price-173526978.html)
- Prior literature summarised by RockawayX: Ante (2019) +7.4% listing day and +9.2% three days after; Yang (2023, CoinDesk) about +41% for Binance; Ouyang (2024, Animoca) about -49% on average after CEX listings, varying by exchange — [RockawayX](https://rockawayx.com/insights/bullish-or-bearish-what-really-happens-when-tokens-list-on-a-cex)
- Academic (AFT 2025 extended abstract; full paper SSRN 4715718): listings on both Coinbase and Binance see significantly positive short-term abnormal returns, significantly larger on Coinbase; Binance is more likely to list coins its venture arm invested in that have weaker fundamentals, and "the apparent agency friction does not seem to be corrected by market forces"; across an 80-exchange regulation index, stricter regulation predicts higher short-term listing returns. No magnitudes in the abstract — [Li, Luo, Wang, Wei, "Cryptocurrency Exchange Listings"](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol354-aft2025/html/LIPIcs.AFT.2025.11/LIPIcs.AFT.2025.11.html)

**Pre-announcement run-up (informed trading)**
- Nov 2017–Feb 2022, hourly prices for 7 days before each announcement: Coinbase (118 analysed, 100 after outlier exclusion) median ratio of the last pre-announcement hour to the price 7 days earlier 1.226 (+22.6%), mean 1.243; Binance (167 analysed, 152 after exclusion) median 1.051 (+5.1%), mean 1.056; BTC over the same windows median 0.992–1.003. 15.3% of Coinbase and 9% of Binance listings more than doubled in the 7 days before announcement — [Wu Blockchain investigation](https://wublock.substack.com/p/investigation-reportis-there-insider)
- Ante finds significant positive CAARs in (-3,-1) of +5.0% across the full sample and concludes "information about listing events likely leak"; 15 of 22 exchanges announce listings on the same day they open trading, seven the day before; Coinbase announces much earlier — [Ante 2019](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Exploring-Market-Reactions-to-Exchange-Listings-of-Cryptocurrencies-BRL-working-paper3.pdf)

**Coinbase listings (relevant as a daily-horizon event on coins already on Binance)**
- Coin Metrics (2018–2021 data): new Coinbase markets posted average and median performance between -1% and +14% against USD, BTC and ETH from 10 days before to 10 days after the announcement; "exploration" announcements skew toward the prevailing market trend — [Cointelegraph on Coin Metrics](https://cointelegraph.com/news/coinmetrics-finds-the-coinbase-effect-is-actually-pretty-lame); Messari's older figure was an average +91% with a -32% to +645% range — same source

- Secondary and unverified: lead time from roadmap appearance to live trading "typically 48–72 hours" — [The Tie 2026 summary via search](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026); MEXC-syndicated pages claim a 68% listing rate and a 70–120 day average timeline with internal date inconsistencies — [example](https://www.mexc.com/news/958582). These conflict; treat as unknown.
- 42 non-stablecoin tokens listed by Binance and Coinbase in one year: about 80% rose more than 10% at some point after announcement (gains short-lived) — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/major-exchange-listings-coinbase-binance) (page blocked full fetch; figure from search summary)

**Upbit effect (the big Korean pump on coins already on Binance)**
- Only event-study numbers: Upbit day +1 CAAR -2.9% (20% positive), (+1,+3) -2.4%, pre-window negative (15 events, pre-2020) — [Ante 2019](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Exploring-Market-Reactions-to-Exchange-Listings-of-Cryptocurrencies-BRL-working-paper3.pdf)
- Jan 2024–Jul 2025 Korean listings (trimmed means, top and bottom 25% excluded): Upbit had the lowest average Day-1 closing price of the Korean venues ("large user base and concentrated sell-offs by speculators"), rebounded by Day 7 and outperformed by Day 30; Bithumb had the best Day 1 but the largest swings and fading gains. Exact percentages only in charts. A $10,000 rise in BTC's average price associated with about 1.19 more listings per month across exchanges. Upbit market share fell from ~86% (2021) to ~70% (Feb 2025); KRW trading about $663B Jan–2025-to-date, world's second-largest fiat market — [TechFlow, "Decoding Data: The Listing Black Box of Korean Exchanges"](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/27889)
- 2025 cohort outcome: Upbit added 59 KRW pairs in 2025 (Bithumb 144); $100 in each at the listing-day close held to 11 Mar 2026 returned -69.5% on average, median -80.9%; 46% of tokens sit at -75% to -90%, 9 (15%) lost more than 90%; only KITE (+232.8%) and BARD (+9.3%) were profitable. Binance's 2025 cohort (92 tokens, Messari chart) returned -71.7% on the same basis — [Blockmedia, "Key Takeaways from Upbit's New KRW Listings in 2025"](https://www.blockmedia.co.kr/research-english/1064579) and [4Pillars, "2025 Korean CEX Listings Post-Mortem"](https://research.4pillars.io/en/research/2025-korean-cex-listings-post-mortem)
- Case reports of the minutes-scale spike on Binance-listed coins after an Upbit notice: CAP +34.5% "in one minute" (CoinMarketCap cautioned it cannot isolate the Upbit effect) — [BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/e5663b55-0efb-49b8-9fd7-1cb7db04d3cd); ENA +19.27% then partial give-back the same day — [Tiger/BeInCrypto](https://www.itiger.com/news/2550398425); MANTRA (OM) +15.7% — [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/mantra-om-upbit-listing-price-surge/); TRUMP +20% — [FXEmpire](https://fxempire.com/forecasts/article/official-trump-coin-surges-over-20-following-upbit-listing-1497574); TOSHI ~+40% — [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/cryptocurrencies/news/toshi-memecoin-soars-over-40-after-upbit-listing-sparks-buying-frenzy-202509170512); Infinit (IN) +121% intraday — [Cryptometer](https://www.cryptometer.io/news/upbit-lists-infinit-in-with-krw-pair-token-surges-over-120-following-announcement/); USELESS +30% in a day — [Gate](https://www.gate.com/blog/useless-lists-on-upbit-and-rises-over-30-in-a-day-how-is-the-korean-market-fueling-the-meme-craze)
- Presto Research: the Upbit pump "is usually short-lived and tends to be a one-time event rather than a sustainable trend"; Kimchi premium typically 2–3%, up to ~14% in bullish periods — [Presto Labs](https://www.prestolabs.io/research/state-of-the-korean-crypto-market)
- Vendor marketing (unverified, single source): "surge 50–100% or more within minutes" and an actionable window "measured in seconds" — [CryptoListing.ws blog](https://cryptolisting.ws/blog/upbit-listing-effect-kimchi-premium/); the same vendor's alert page claims a median +32.6% best price within 24 hours across 62 Upbit listings since April 2026 with no data source — [CryptoListing.ws alert page](https://cryptolisting.ws/upbit-listing-alert/)

### Inferences
- Gross vs net at the app's horizon: using Empirica's 2024 Binance cohort (+2.78% next day from the first-day close, +0.40% at week 1), a next-open entry on the first daily close after listing nets roughly +2.0% to +2.4% after 0.40–0.75% costs if exited the next day, and about zero to negative by week 1; Ante's post-event (+1,+3) of -1.5% (full sample) and -6.3% (Binance) implies a negative net return for the same trade. The newly listed coin is in any case excluded by the app's 90-day rule, so the only actionable case is an existing Binance coin getting a second-venue listing.
- For a Binance-listed coin added to Coinbase or Upbit, the evidence says the capturable move is the pre-announcement run-up (which cannot be known) and the first minutes after the notice. With daily or 4-hour closes the app would enter after the spike, into Ante's negative day +1/(+1,+3) window and the Tie's two-week mean reversion. Hourly closes catch the tail only when the notice lands shortly before the hour; the expected edge after a 0.40–0.75% round trip is not demonstrated by any source found. Treat listings on Coinbase/Upbit as (a) a reason not to buy a pump candle that day and (b) a possible exit prompt on an open position, pending the app's own event backtest.
- The Techflow observation that Upbit's Day-1 close is the trough with a Day-7 rebound is the one hint of a contrarian daily entry (buy the Day-1 close of a Korean listing), but it is unquantified and based on new listings, and the 2025 cohort median of -80.9% from the Day-1 close says any such bounce is a short trade inside a long decline.
- The 2024 "pump-then-dump" vendor statistics (87% pump, 98% dump) are peak-to-trough and from unfixed windows; they confirm direction, not a tradable size.
- Pre-announcement run-ups of +5% (Binance) and +23% (Coinbase) over 7 days before announcement are consistent with leaks; an app cannot use them without inside information, but they explain why post-announcement returns are weak.
- Binance Alpha and futures-first listings mostly concern coins the 90-day rule already excludes; the PANews numbers suggest a selection effect (larger tokens get futures) rather than a strategy.

### Gaps
- No study quantifies the Upbit effect specifically for coins already listed on Binance at fixed horizons (1h, 24h, 7d) with costs; only Ante's 15 pre-2020 events and 2025 case reports exist. The JKSCI paper may have numbers but only the abstract was reachable.
- The Tie's 2026 cohort medians by exchange and by day exist only as charts; no per-exchange return figures were available in text.
- Nothing found on the Seed Tag (introduced 2023) as a performance predictor.
- No study of the "futures-first then spot" sequence measured from the spot-listing date.
- Latency of the Binance CMS endpoint versus the website or the key-gated WebSocket was not measured; no official RSS feed was found. Anti-bot behaviour toward phone clients is unknown (SSRN, Coinbase, BeInCrypto and CryptoRank blocked this run's fetches).
- Coinbase roadmap-to-listing lead time: sources conflict (48–72 hours vs 70–120 days), both unverified.
- Full-paper magnitudes for Li et al. (SSRN 4715718) could not be fetched.

## Key question 2: Delisting announcements (Binance, Upbit, Coinbase) and whether a delisting veto is worth coding

### Takeaway
No aggregate event study of exchange delistings was found; the evidence is case reports, but they are uniformly large and immediate (roughly -15% to -57% within a day for thinner tokens), and Binance and Upbit both publish the notice days to weeks before trading stops. An "exit on delisting notice, never enter" veto costs nothing and the documented downside it avoids is far larger than the fee frame.

- Academic context: an event study of major listing, delisting and airdrop announcements plus SEC enforcements over (-5,+10) finds more significant abnormal returns for negative events than positive ones (Öget 2022); only a search summary was available, no URL to the paper — see Gaps.

### Inferences
- The veto is asymmetric: a Binance spot delisting notice precedes the stop by days to two weeks, the first-day drop is typically 15–40% (cases), and the app's long-only book has no way to profit from it. Coding "exit at the next open after a delisting notice for the held coin; block entries for coins on any delisting or Upbit caution list" is supported by every case found.
- The Upbit "caution designation" (거래 유의 종목 지정) and Binance's "Monitoring Tag" are earlier warnings; treating them as entry vetoes is reasonable but unquantified.
- Removal of a single trading pair (e.g. a BTC- or FDUSD-quoted pair) is noise for a USDT-pair app; the parser should distinguish "Will Delist <coin>" from "Removal of Spot Trading Pairs".

### Gaps
- No aggregate study with sample, window and average drop for Binance, Upbit or Coinbase delistings 2023–2026. The Öget (2022) paper could not be located by URL.
- Recovery after the delisting drop (does the coin bounce before the actual delist date?) is unmeasured; cases such as Aragon returning to initial levels suggest heterogeneity ([Tangem](https://tangem.com/en/news/markets/18619-binance-delists-tokens-triggers-sharp-price-drops/)).

## Key question 3: Token unlocks

### Takeaway
Every study found (Keyrock 16,000+ events, a 52-event Binance study, a 236-event benchmarked study, Messari's 619 events) agrees that large cliff unlocks are negative for price, with the decline starting 14–30 days before and concentrated within about a week after; the threshold where it becomes statistically significant is roughly 5% of circulating supply, and small or linear unlocks (<2% of supply, or <1 day of volume) show nothing. For a long-only app this is a veto window (about -14 to +7 days) that avoids a median loss of roughly 5–16% per event; profiting from it needs a short. The free unlock calendar on DefiLlama has moved behind a $300/month API.

### Cited Findings
- Keyrock (December 2024; more than 16,000 unlock events): about 90% of unlocks create negative price pressure "regardless of the size, type, or recipient"; declines often begin about 30 days before the event; unlocks of 5–10% of supply produce drops roughly 2.4x more severe than smaller releases; team unlocks average about -25%, ecosystem-development unlocks +1.18% (the only positive category). Primary report not reachable; figures via [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/keyrock-research-token-unlocks/), [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/90-of-token-unlocks-drive-prices-down-declines-begin-a-month-ahead/), [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/token-unlocks-almost-always-negative-for-price-keyrocks-study-reveals/)
- SSRN preprint (2026), 52 unlock events on Binance-listed tokens: 46 of 52 (88.5%) show negative returns within 72 hours of the unlock, mean -16.97%; described as a cross-sectional analysis with asymmetric impact; page blocked direct fetch, figures via search summary — [Kim, "The 72-Hour Shock?"](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6632838)
- 236-event benchmarked study (source not named in the compilation): one-month post-unlock median -16.26% vs BTC (mean -8.10%, n=236) and -4.85% vs matched peers (n=221); pre-event one-month median -14.7% (p<0.001, n=164), two-week median -9.1%; 72.5% of events closed lower one month later — [Streamflow compilation](https://streamflow.finance/blog/token-unlock-report)
- Messari (619 events, 41 assets, via Gate): most events increase circulating supply by less than 2.5%, fewer than half exceed 5%; downside statistically significant only above 5% of circulating supply, no material correlation below 2%; weakness concentrated within the seven days around the event — [Streamflow compilation](https://streamflow.finance/blog/token-unlock-report)
- The Tie (12,350 priced unlock events, 2026 YTD to Aug 19): median event equals 0.20% of average daily volume; 95.6% of events are below one day's volume; 544 events exceeded a day's volume — [Streamflow compilation](https://streamflow.finance/blog/token-unlock-report)
- Unattributed in the same compilation: early-stage, thin-float tokens median -16% vs similar-cap peers and -14.8% vs age-matched peers; established tokens show no significant effect; non-insider tranches of unlocks ≥10% of market cap median -26% (n=131); a timeline of derivatives hedging and negative funding at days -30 to -14, spot selling at -14 to 0 with the median token down about 9%, stabilisation after +7 — [Streamflow](https://streamflow.finance/blog/token-unlock-report)
- Tokenomist's own finding (via search summary): the effect is concentrated in thin-float tokens and largely priced in before the unlock date, with no significant impact on established high-liquidity tokens — [Streamflow, "Do Token Unlocks Cause Price Drops?"](https://streamflow.finance/blog/do-token-unlocks-cause-price-drops). The Tokenomist PDF itself exceeded the fetch size limit — [Tokenomist PDF](https://space.tokenomist.ai/assets/abb63176ec1dad3fc93187102b8b0986.pdf)
- Sept 2026 example: OPN's prior unlocks since March 2026 were followed by declines averaging -30.1% within 14 days — [Streamflow](https://streamflow.finance/blog/token-unlock-report)

**Data sources (verified)**
- DefiLlama: the free endpoint list (base `https://api.llama.fi`, "no authentication required", 31 GET endpoints) contains no emissions or unlocks route — [llms-free.txt](https://api-docs.defillama.com/llms-free.txt). Unlocks live only in the Pro API: `GET https://pro-api.llama.fi/{API_KEY}/api/emissions` (array of tokens with `token, name, circSupply, circSupply30d, totalLocked, maxSupply, gecko_id, events, nextEvent, unlocksPerDay, mcap`) and `GET .../api/emission/{protocol}` — [llms-pro.txt](https://api-docs.defillama.com/llms-pro.txt). The legacy `https://api.llama.fi/emissions` now returns HTTP 402 Payment Required — [endpoint](https://api.llama.fi/emissions). API plan $300/month or $3,000/year, 1,000 requests/minute, 1M calls/month, $0.60 per extra 1,000 — [DefiLlama pricing](https://docs.llama.fi/pro-api)
- Tokenomist: API is a paid product with a free trial by request form; trial rate limit 60 requests/minute (same as paid), unlock-events endpoint returns 1 year backward; personal/non-commercial, non-redistribution — [Tokenomist tier overviews](https://docs.tokenomist.ai/tier-overviews) and [pricing](https://www.tokenomist.ai/pricing)

### Inferences
- A long-only veto "no entry from T-14 to T+7 around an unlock >2–5% of circulating supply or >1 day of volume" removes exposure to a median -9% pre-event and -5% to -16% post-event move; because 95.6% of unlock events are below one day's volume, the filter will fire rarely and mostly on mid/small caps where the app's 0.40–0.75% fee tier applies.
- Shorting into unlocks (the profitable side) requires perpetual futures: extension.
- Without DefiLlama's free feed, the cheapest free path is a Tokenomist trial key (60 rpm, 1 year back) or scraping; CoinMarketCal and CryptoRank free tiers could not be verified (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- Keyrock's methodology (how impact is measured, period, benchmark) is unavailable; one secondary source describes the sample as "~40 major tokens" and another as "16,000+ events", which cannot both be exact.
- The 236-event benchmarked study is unattributed in the only source that carries it.
- CoinMarketCal API docs (403 / DNS failure) and CryptoRank API page (403) could not be fetched; free-tier quotas unknown.
- No out-of-sample test of an unlock-avoidance rule net of fees was found.

## Key question 4: Airdrops and TGEs, and whether the app's 90-day exclusion is right

### Takeaway
2025 launch cohorts were disastrous on every measure (85% below launch price at year-end; two-thirds down within 7 days; median -71% to -82%), with the damage front-loaded into the first week and continuing to at least six months. The evidence supports a long exclusion of new coins; 90 days captures most of the early damage, and the data suggests a 180-day window would exclude more losers at the cost of universe size.

### Cited Findings
- Arrakis Finance (80-page analysis of 125 TGEs in 2025, plus 25 founder interviews): about 85% of tokens finished 2025 below their initial price; nearly two-thirds were already down within seven days; only 9.4% of those down in week one later recovered to launch price; up to 80% of airdrop recipients sold on day one; "early sell pressure, not market timing, largely determined whether new tokens thrived in 2025" — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/research-and-opinion/only-1-in-10-weak-token-launches-recovered-in-2025-arrakis)
- Memento Research (118 TGEs in 2025): 100 (84.7%) trade below their listing FDV; median FDV change -71%, median market-cap change -67%; 65% lost around half, over half are down 70% or more — [DigitalToday](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/84494/new-coins-since-2024-most-fail-to-hold-tge-price) and [MEXC news](https://www.mexc.com/news/338555)
- Delphi Digital "State of the Token Market" (652 tokens listed on CEXs since 2025): median return -82%; more than half lost over 80%; the average token spent about 70% of the post-listing period below its TGE price — [DigitalToday](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/84494/new-coins-since-2024-most-fail-to-hold-tge-price)
- CryptoRank (113 tokens launched 2024–2026 with $100M+ market cap): only 8 traded above TGE price; median return -95.7% — [MEXC news summary](https://www.mexc.com/news/338555); CryptoRank's own airdrop sell-vs-hold article was blocked (403) — [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io/insights/analytics/should-you-sell-or-hold-your-airdrop)
- Airdrop cases: seven of eight large airdrops lost 92–99% after launch; Hyperliquid was the exception, up roughly 35x from claim day — [BeInCrypto (ES)](https://es.beincrypto.com/analisis-airdrop-estrategia-declive-token/)
- Older but large: Jump Crypto (3,759 tokens listed mid-2013 to end-2021) found about 80% of new tokens underwater relative to BTC within a year with average relative returns near zero, and the majority underperforming within the first month — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-04-28/most-new-tokens-are-underwater-relative-to-bitcoin-within-a-year)
- Momtaz (European Journal of Finance 2021), ICO tokens after listing: for holding periods of 1–24 months the median token depreciates by 30%; mean buy-and-hold positive, median negative — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=3169682)
- Binance 2024 listings from first-day close: -1.76% at 1 month, -22.66% at 3 months, -37.64% at 6 months; 5.5% positive at 6 months — [Empirica](https://empirica.io/blog/the-binance-effect-a-7-year-analysis-for-token-founders/)
- 2025 cohorts from day-1 close to 11 Mar 2026: Upbit median -80.9%, Bithumb median -82.1%, Binance -71.7% portfolio — [4Pillars](https://research.4pillars.io/en/research/2025-korean-cex-listings-post-mortem)
- Small-cap listings' underperformance "widens at two to three months" — [The Tie 2026](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026)
- Binance Alpha airdrop tokens: 91 of 144 fell after Alpha listing — [ChainCatcher/PANews](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2205435)

### Inferences
- The 90-day cutoff is supported: the first week carries the heaviest selling (Arrakis two-thirds down by day 7; 80% of airdrop recipients selling day one), and most of Jump's underperformers were already underperforming within a month. But the Binance 2024 cohort drops another ~15 points between months 3 and 6 (-22.66% to -37.64%) and the Tie notes widening small-cap underperformance at 2–3 months, so a 180-day exclusion would avoid more losses. The trade-off is universe size, which the app can measure directly from its ledger.
- Points-farming returns are a pre-TGE activity and irrelevant to a spot app; "sell the airdrop" is the documented base rate (7 of 8 large airdrops down 92–99%; Hyperliquid the exception).

### Gaps
- No source gives 7/30/90/180-day return tables for 2025 TGEs; the figures are year-end or snapshot-based.
- No study isolates airdrop-distributed tokens from other TGEs at fixed horizons in 2024–2026.
- The primary Arrakis, Memento, Delphi and CryptoRank reports were not directly reachable; figures come from reputable coverage.

## Key question 6: Copy trading and whale following

### Takeaway
Independent evidence says followers, not leaders, lose: academic work finds copied trades lose more in downturns and copy trading raises risk-taking; the only multi-exchange 2025 dataset found (vendor-published) has 97% of lead traders profitable but only 48% of followers, with MEXC followers net negative; on Hyperliquid 75–83% of addresses are unprofitable. Exchange-published success rates (Bitget 93%) are self-reported marketing. Following whales from a daily-close spot app is not supported by any measured edge and would need Hyperliquid/perp data: extension.

### Cited Findings
- Pelster & Hofmann (2018, Journal of Banking & Finance 94, 75–88): losses are usually higher for copied trades when returns are negative; copied investors are more prone to the disposition effect — [Wikipedia summary with citation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copy_trading)
- Apesteguia, Oechssler & Weidenholzer (2020, Management Science 66(12)): success information alone may increase risk-taking and the option to copy directly increases it further; risk aversion drives who copies — [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copy_trading); journal page blocked fetch — [INFORMS](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2019.3508)
- Liu et al. (2014, PLOS ONE): copied trades are more likely profitable than standard trades but earn lower returns than successful regular trades — [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copy_trading)
- 2012 MIT/eToro study (press-release provenance): guided copying fared 6–10% better than manual trading and 4% better than copying random investors — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copy_trading)
- Tallinn University thesis, 28 eToro copy-portfolios 2017–2020: 21 positive alpha, 7 negative — [TalTech](https://digikogu.taltech.ee/en/Download/9c402020-d23e-447e-bcba-e41823baa02d); a University of Vienna thesis concludes copy portfolios cannot consistently be said to outperform the S&P 500 — [uTheses](https://utheses-gateway.univie.ac.at/api/document/get/file/73534)
- "YieldFund Multi-Exchange Study (2025)", 90 days, 100,000+ copy-trading outcomes on Binance, Bybit, MEXC (as reported by a competitor exchange blog; methodology not described): follower win rate Binance 66.50%, Bybit 43.65%, MEXC 57.79%; net follower PnL Binance +572,979 USDT, Bybit +346,106, MEXC -210,040; leaders with positive follower returns 72.45% / 40.34% / under 50%; only 48.48% of followers profitable overall while 97% of lead traders were profitable; leaders typically charge 10–30% profit share — [KuCoin blog](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/is-crypto-copytrading-profitable-in-2026)
- Bitget self-reported, H1 2023: 93% of futures and 82% of spot copy traders "made profits" — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/gen-z-dominates-crypto-copy-trading-space-85-make-profit/)
- Hyperliquid, Hyperdash data June 2025: 166 of 1,000 analysed traders profitable (16.6%); average daily PnL across users a loss of $5,600; 170 traders earned over $10M and 1,589 at least $1M; roughly 20% of users received 80% of the airdrop; selection and PnL method undocumented — [The Coin Republic](https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2025/06/16/hyperliquid-crypto-only-166-of-1000-traders-profitable-whats-going-on/)
- Hyperliquid, May 2026 (Marsbit via HTX, secondary): approximately 75% of addresses are losing money; profitable addresses run systematic high-frequency strategies or large asymmetric bets — [HTX](https://www.htx.com/news/data-75-of-traders-on-hyperliquid-are-losing-money-what-are-R2h1lIXH/)
- Whale-tracker caveats: a vault page notes its PnL differs from Hyperliquid's site method and strips deposits/withdrawals, so raw account PnL can overstate results — [TradingStrategy.ai](https://tradingstrategy.ai/trading-view/vaults/hypertwin-blue-whale-2); KOL accounts with losses over $10M and high win rates on low trade counts — [Odaily](https://www.odaily.news/en/post/5206026)
- UK FCA (2014–2015) treated copy-trading firms as investment managers; ESMA's MiFID Q&A treats copy trading as automatic execution of signals — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copy_trading)

### Inferences
- The structural problem is timing and selection: followers enter after the leader's fill, pay 10–30% of profits plus fees, and leaderboards show survivors. A daily-close app that mirrors a whale's position seen on-chain would add a further lag of up to a day; no source measured a positive net return for that.
- The only defensible use of whale data is as a contrarian or crowding indicator, which would need the Hyperliquid info API or Arkham labels: extension requiring perpetual-futures positioning data.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study of crypto copy trading on Binance/Bybit/Bitget; the YieldFund study's methodology and survivorship treatment are undocumented, and its blended 48.48% does not match the simple average of the three win rates.
- No follower-level (not leader-level) return data for Hyperliquid or Arkham-tracked whales.
- Hyperliquid public API (api.hyperliquid.xyz/info) keyless status was not verified this run.

## Key question 7: Prediction markets (Polymarket, Kalshi) as spot signals

### Takeaway
The only direct test found (258 Polymarket BTC contracts, hourly, Oct 2024–Mar 2026) finds no Granger-predictive content for next-hour BTC returns and shows a closed-form barrier model explains over 95% of contract prices from spot, strike, time and volatility; prediction markets repackage spot and options information rather than lead it, and 5-minute markets create settlement-manipulation incentives. Both Polymarket's and Kalshi's market APIs are keyless.

### Cited Findings
- Olsen (Aalborg master's thesis, submitted 2026-05-31): 258 monthly Bitcoin price-target (touch) contracts, hourly, Oct 2024–Mar 2026, with replication on 252 ETH contracts; Mincer-Zarnowitz tests cannot reject unbiased calibration; per-contract Granger tests "find no detectable predictive content for next-hour Bitcoin returns"; a one-touch barrier model explains over 95% of cross-sectional price variation under implied and realized volatility; Polymarket prices sit below the implied-vol benchmark, attributed to the variance risk premium — [Aalborg University](https://projekter.aau.dk/the-informational-content-of-prediction-markets-evidence-from-polymarket-bitcoin-contracts-628468cf.html)
- Akey et al. (ESSEC, April 2026): Polymarket prices broadly track option-implied benchmarks with systematic gaps driven by behavioural factors and frictions, largest in tail events and high volatility — [RePEc listing](https://ideas.repec.org/p/arx/papers/2606.19517.html)
- Portnaya (2026 working paper): for the September 2023 BTC contract the mean pricing gap vs Binance options was 5.6 percentage points across 214 hourly observations — via [Capital Spectator](https://www.capitalspectator.com/2026/04/24/)
- Stanford/SMU (July 2026): 5-minute Bitcoin markets settle on Chainlink feeds at window end, creating incentives to influence spot just before settlement; estimated transfer about $1.28M from ordinary traders to manipulators in the sample (one outlet reports $8.2M, unverified) — [SMU](https://business.smu.edu.sg/news/stanford-study-pins-polymarket-bitcoin-settlement-manipulation-contract-design) and [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/stanford-study-5-minute-bitcoin-prediction-markets-enable-settlement-manipulation)

**Data (verified)**
- Polymarket Gamma API returned JSON without a key: `https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/markets?limit=2&active=true` with `question, outcomePrices, volume, liquidity, endDate, clobTokenIds` (note: the `tag=crypto` parameter was ignored and political markets were returned; tag filtering needs the tag id) — [endpoint](https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/markets?limit=2&active=true&tag=crypto)
- Kalshi trade API v2 returned JSON without a key: `https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/markets?limit=2&status=open&series_ticker=KXBTCD` → daily "Bitcoin price on Oct 22, 2026?" strike markets with `ticker, title, yes_bid, yes_ask, last_price, volume_fp, volume_24h_fp, close_time` — [endpoint](https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/markets?limit=2&status=open&series_ticker=KXBTCD)

### Inferences
- Reading Polymarket/Kalshi BTC probabilities adds nothing beyond spot and Deribit implied volatility at the hourly horizon; the one documented edge (settlement manipulation of 5-minute markets) is adversarial, sub-minute and not a spot strategy.

### Gaps
- No study tests Kalshi crypto markets as a signal.
- No daily-horizon test (the thesis is hourly) and no test of altcoin contracts.

## Key question 8: Macro and calendar effects (FOMC, CPI, US session, Uptober, halving, expiry Fridays, weekends)

### Takeaway
Scheduled macro days move Bitcoin's volatility, not its average return: a 2020–2025 event study (62 CPI, 42 FOMC days) finds significant abnormal volatility and no significant abnormal return in any window or sub-group, and the NY Fed found Bitcoin orthogonal to macro news; the renewed 2026 CPI sensitivity is confined to the first hour. Session, weekend and "Uptober" tilts exist in-sample but are decaying, untested after costs, or rest on 10–13 observations, which agrees with the app's earlier finding that hour-of-day and overnight tilts decayed.

### Cited Findings
**FOMC and CPI**
- Nazaruk (KSE MA thesis, 2025), 2020–2025, 62 CPI and 42 FOMC events after removing overlaps, market model vs S&P 500 with abnormal volatility (Parkinson and Rogers-Satchell): CPI days show abnormal volatility from two days before, significant +0.93 p.p. on τ=0 (p=0.004) and +0.70 p.p. on τ=+1 (p=0.039); "Cumulative abnormal returns are insignificant on all days in the event window"; for FOMC "significant rise in volatility and no price drift" (AVOL +0.73 p.p.); by CPI direction and by hike/cut/unchanged, "across all groups and windows, CARs are insignificant"; the author's recommendation is to raise VaR/ES inputs on CPI days, not to trade direction — [KSE thesis PDF](https://kse.ua/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/illia-nazaruk_268722_assignsubmission_file_nazaruk_final_thesis.pdf)
- Benigno & Rosa (NY Fed Staff Report 1052, Feb 2023), intraday event study: Bitcoin is "orthogonal to monetary and macroeconomic news," unlike other U.S. asset classes — [NY Fed](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr1052)
- Block Scholes (Sept 2026): regression of BTC first-hour return on the CPI surprise (headline YoY minus consensus) explains ~25% of variance over the full sample since 2020, 52% at the Dec 2021–Nov 2023 peak (β ≈ -13% per pp), and nearly 80% on a rolling 12-release window in 2026 with a smaller β ≈ -3.2% per pp (a hot print lowers BTC); the relationship "is so far confined to the one-hour horizon" and absent at 4–24 hours — [Block Scholes](https://www.blockscholes.com/institutional-research/is-bitcoin-showing-greater-sensitivity-to-us-cpi-releases-again)
- Mahmoudi (arXiv, Oct 2023), monthly MSM-VAR 2010–2023: monetary-policy uncertainty lowers BTC returns in both regimes (-0.028 low-vol, -0.44 high-vol); no day-level FOMC effect reported — [arXiv 2311.10739](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.10739)
- Finnish event study of 49 FOMC dates with a [-1,+1] window and the CoinDesk Market Index benchmark exists but was blocked by an access check — [Osuva](https://osuva.uwasa.fi/bitstreams/50105245-0992-4f34-b16a-b9e7888d49d7/download)
- Analyst lore: "Bitcoin tends to fall before FOMC and recover after" (anecdotal) — [Benzinga](https://benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/24/06/39269869/bitcoin-dipping-the-usual-inflation-data-scam-dump-or-a-strategic-buying-opportunity)

**US session, overnight, weekend**
- Quantpedia (Nov 2024), hourly Gemini data 2015-10-08 to 2024-10-15, "daily" = 10:00–16:00 ET on NYSE days: daily-session performance was strong until ~2021 and "plateaued over the last three years"; since 2021 most BTC return comes overnight; most positive performance falls on Friday close→Monday open, Monday→Tuesday and Tuesday→Wednesday ("a Weekend Effect exists"); a MAX(10) rule (buy at NYSE close when BTC is at a 10-day high, hold one trading day) returned "over 35%" out of sample (from Oct 2021) with ~-12% max drawdown; no transaction costs, arbitrary split, out-of-sample weaker than in-sample — [Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/how-to-profitably-trade-bitcoins-overnight-sessions/)
- Five-minute data Jan 2018–Dec 2023: average overnight return 0.093% vs -0.029% during US hours; but the pattern "hasn't been the case for most of the past year"; a Bloomberg Intelligence note puts IBIT's average close-to-open gap near 2%; the NGHT overnight-BTC ETF had $24.37M of assets and earlier night-stock ETFs closed after underperforming — [Yahoo Finance/Investopedia](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/theres-proposed-etf-people-think-163207083.html)
- Kaiko (June 2024): weekend share of BTC volume fell to ~16% in 2024 from up to 28% in 2019, the lowest ever, with ETF-hours and the benchmark fixing window taking a larger share — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2024-06-30-bitcoin-sees-all-time-low-weekend-trading-levels-following-etf-approvals-kaiko-302705)
- Volmex Labs (Aug 2024): after spot ETFs, volume and realized volatility rose mainly on weekdays; weekend implied-vol drop not statistically significant — [Volmex PDF](https://volmex.finance/The%20Impact%20of%20Spot%20Bitcoin%20ETFs.pdf)
- Full-sample day-of-week (not ETF-specific): Monday–Wednesday above average, Thursday and weekends below — [Deutsche Digital Assets](https://deutschedigitalassets.com/insights/news/bitcoin-seasonality-sell-in-may-and-go-away/); CXO Advisory's 2014–Jul 2025 weekday study is paywalled — [CXO](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/?p=41865)

**Uptober, halving, expiries**
- BIT (2013–2025): October mean +19.92%, median +14.71%, 10 of 13 positive; 2016–2025 mean +17.8%, 80% positive; October 2025 closed -3.7%, ending six straight positive Octobers (2019–2024); "none of those theories is proven" and naming the pattern pulls buying forward — [BIT Knowledge Hub](https://www.bit.com/knowledge-hub/bitcoin-seasonality-uptober)
- Delphi Digital: October +22% average, +23.5% median since 2013; only 2014 and 2018 negative in the prior ten — [Delphi](https://members.delphidigital.io/feed/will-october-be-a-bunch-of-hocus-pocus); QCP: average +22.9% in eight of the last nine Octobers — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/314325/qcp-capital-forecasts-potential-bitcoin-rally-in-october-after-a-near-term-price-dip); CryptoQuant/XWIN monthly averages: Aug -0.54%, Sep -4.16%, Oct +29.9%, Nov +37.5%, Dec +4.75% — [ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/analysts-elucidate-the-pattern-of-seasonal-rallies-in-q4/amp)
- Grayscale's Pandl: November's 46% average is heavily influenced by 2013; liquidity and macro matter more than the calendar — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/248314/september-effect-bitcoin-wall-street?amp=1)
- Halving cycle: 2025 described as a muted "fourth year" with YTD gain just under 20%; no statistical test — [BM Pro](https://bmpro.substack.com/p/is-uptober-about-to-deliver-again)
- Deribit expiries: a Copper analyst said broader data "does not suggest that the bulk expiry of options contracts affects the price" — [Decrypt 2021](https://decrypt.co/72196/about-2-1-billion-in-bitcoin-options-expired-today); Decrypt 2025: "big expiries don't automatically move Bitcoin's price" — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/376752/billion-bitcoin-options-expire-what-it-means); CoinDesk notes historical support for a pre-expiry rally depending on settlement size — [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/bitcoin-options-market-expiry-may); the max-pain curve is "reasonably flat at the bottom" — [Stockhead](https://stockhead.com.au/cryptocurrency/bitcoins-max-pain-crypto-market-seems-intent-on-inflicting-maximum-damage-on-options-buyers)

### Inferences
- Net of costs at the app's horizons: no macro-day direction rule is supported (CARs insignificant in 104 events; NY Fed orthogonality); CPI/FOMC are volatility events, useful only for widening stops or pausing entries on the daily close before the release. A 1-hour CPI-surprise reaction of ~-0.3% per 0.1pp (2026 β) is below the 0.25% BTC round trip for a typical 0.1pp surprise and is gone by the 4-hour close.
- Weekend/overnight tilts: in-sample evidence through 2024 with no costs; the Investopedia note that it failed for most of the past year and Quantpedia's weaker out-of-sample corroborate the app's own decay finding. Per-trade magnitudes (overnight 0.093%/day in 2018–2023) are below the fee frame.
- Uptober is a 10–13 observation seasonal with one fresh miss (2025); it cannot be a signal, at most a very weak prior.
- Expiry-Friday and halving effects: lore, no test found.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 study of weekend returns (as opposed to volume/volatility) in the ETF era; CXO's weekday study is paywalled.
- Finnish FOMC event study (49 dates) blocked; the KSE thesis is a student work (though with transparent method and 104 events).
- No quantitative test of quarter-end/monthly Deribit expiry drift.
- No test of US-equity-open/close effects on altcoins (sources are BTC-only).

## Key question 9: Binance's own retail products as signals or strategies (Launchpool, HODLer airdrops, Alpha points, Trading Bots)

### Takeaway
Only Binance's own marketing numbers exist: BNB holders using its reward programs earned a combined 177% from Jan 2024 to Mar 2025, of which 104 points were BNB price appreciation and ~20 points HODLer/MegaDrop airdrops; no independent study isolates a Launchpool or HODLer-airdrop effect on BNB's price, and no published usage or performance statistics for Binance Trading Bots were found. Nothing here is a tested signal.

### Cited Findings
- Binance-reported: BNB holders in reward programs earned 177% combined from January 2024 to March 2025 (averaging 11.8% monthly); about 104% from BNB rising from $313 to $640; MegaDrop and HODLer Airdrops added 19.7% for participants who caught every drop; Launchpool average APY 84% across Launchpools from early 2024 to Q1 2025; token rewards valued at first-day closing prices — [Blockchain.News](https://blockchain.news/zh/news/bnb-177-percent-returns-binance-rewards-launchpool-airdrops)
- HODLer Airdrops launched 2024, eligibility via BNB balance snapshots in Simple Earn with KYC — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/236144/binance-airdrop-bnb-holder-rewards-crypto-projects?amp=1); 65th HODLer Airdrop (Genius Terminal) in May 2026, coverage reports no BNB price effect — [Whale Alert story](https://whale-alert.io/stories/babb017131c665/BNB-price-reclaims-670-after-VanEck-launch-as-Binances-Genius-Terminal-HODLer-airdrop-remains-in-focus)
- Alpha outcomes (see Key question 1): ~10% of Alpha tokens reached spot; 24% of 335 Alpha tokens halved; 91 of 144 Alpha airdrop tokens fell — [The Defiant/DWF](https://thedefiant.io/news/press-releases/dwf-ventures-analyzes-performance-of-binance-alpha-and-spot-listings), [ChainCatcher/PANews](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2205435)
- Trading Bots: Binance launched Trading Bots (spot and futures grid, DCA, rebalancing) in 2023 — [AMBCrypto](https://ambcrypto.com/binance-announces-new-feature-to-bolster-trading-as-bnb-continues-to-record-losses/); Binance Research's 2023 bot report concerned Telegram bots ($190M cumulative volume) not its own products — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/telegram-crypto-bots-gain-momentum-binance-research); user anecdotes on Binance Square: an XRP grid backtest Q1 2025 returned 27.74% vs 0.24% for holding with 98.2% of capital idle at period end; a BTC grid in April 2025 returned 7.35% vs 14.15% buy-and-hold with $947.81 of $1,000 idle — [Binance Square #gridbot](https://www.binance.com/square/hashtag/gridbot)

### Inferences
- The "Binance effect on BNB" is confounded with the 2024–2025 bull market; Binance's own decomposition attributes most of the 177% to price. Launchpool/HODLer announcements as a BNB entry signal are untested.
- Grid-bot anecdotes illustrate the known property (range-bound outperformance, trend underperformance, high idle capital); they are not performance statistics.

### Gaps
- No independent event study of BNB around Launchpool/HODLer announcements.
- No Binance publication of Trading Bots user counts, volumes or aggregate PnL for 2024–2025.
- Nothing on Alpha points as a signal.
