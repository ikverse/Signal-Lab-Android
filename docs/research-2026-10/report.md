# Volatility sizing and vetoes beat new entry signals

Across the 2023 to 2026 evidence on derivatives, on-chain, event-driven, systematic, yield and execution strategies, almost nothing new qualifies as a long-only daily *entry* signal with credible net-of-cost evidence for Binance spot at 0.25 to 0.40% round trip; what qualifies is a change in how Signal Lab's seven existing strategies are sized and gated. The best-supported single step is **volatility scaling of existing trend calls**, which took one-month time-series momentum from a **Sharpe of 0.65 to 1.83** out of sample on 150+ Binance perpetuals net of fees, slippage and funding, and cut drawdown from **-80% to -15%** in an independent spot test. The only peer-reviewed new entry is a cross-sectional composite of 28 trend indicators (JFQA 2025) whose top-100-coin long leg earned **3.1 to 3.4% a week net** of 30 to 50 bps per trade, but its sample ends in May 2022 and its 2018-2022 long leg is not significant on its own. Derivatives data yields one long-side permission (sustained negative 30-day funding: win rates of 83 to 96% against 55 to 70% for random dates, on roughly seven to nine episodes) and two vetoes (high annualised basis per the BIS, and leverage build-ups per K33), but no entry. Event calendars pay only as vetoes: delisting notices precede one-day drops of **15 to 57%** by 3 to 14 days, unlocks above 5% of supply carry median losses of 5 to 16%, and **85% of 2025 token launches** finished below launch price. Tested and found wanting at this horizon: liquidation-flush buying, OI quadrants, CME gap fades (moot since CME went 24/7 on 29 May 2026), whale and smart-money copying, daily exchange netflows, stablecoin supply as a lead, published ETF flows, Fear and Greed, Google Trends, calendar means, machine learning on OHLCV, grid and DCA bots, triangular arbitrage, prediction markets and listing pumps. Funding carry paid **11.9% gross on BTC in 2024, 5.1% in 2025 and 3.0% in 2026 to date**, below a plain stablecoin yield once fees and the capital buffer are counted, and 10 October 2025 showed hedged books turned naked by auto-deleveraging. Going live is technically routine in October 2026 (Ed25519 keys, zero-weight orders, a 5 USDT minimum notional, an idempotent intent log) but legally decisive: Egypt's Law 194/2020 prohibits trading itself, and a genuine 0.5 to 1% net edge is worth **$130 to $2,600 a year on $1,000 to $20,000** at prudent sizing and needs **400 to 1,600 trades** before the ledger can distinguish it from luck.

## Volatility scaling is the one change with two independent net-of-cost tests

The clearest result in the systematic literature is that the edge in crypto trend-following is risk timing rather than return prediction, and that Signal Lab can capture it without adding a single new entry rule. Bui and Nguyen tested 150+ Binance perpetuals on 6-hour bars, calibrated on 2021 and evaluated out of sample from January 2022 to December 2024 with a 4 bps taker fee, volume-based slippage and funding: **volatility-scaled one-month TSMOM at a 10% target earned a Sharpe of 1.83 with annual return 22.8% and maximum drawdown -16.1%**, against **0.65, 18.4% and -34.8%** for the unscaled version, while BTC buy-and-hold over the same window scored 0.17 with a -64.1% drawdown ([arXiv 2602.11708](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11708v1)). The independent Summitward test on daily BTC, ETH and SOL spot closes from January 2017 to June 2025 found the same drawdown effect with a much smaller Sharpe gain: raw trend 0.71 with an -80% drawdown became 0.79 with -15% after 60-day volatility scaling, a plain 200-day BTC long/flat rule reached 0.89 with a -10.4% drawdown, and the authors concluded that **"the trend signal added about 0.05 Sharpe"** on top of volatility management ([Summitward](https://summitward.com/learn/crypto-trend-following)). The multi-asset literature says the same thing in a different register: Zakamulin's 1995-2025 long-only trend Sharpe of 0.74 against buy-and-hold 0.61 is matched by model-implied volatility timing at 0.76, and Moskowitz and co-authors find that a **binary in/out rule is the worst way to shape a trend signal**, beaten by both linear and nonlinear position scaling ([Marrazzo review](https://antoniomarrazzo.substack.com/p/trend-following-mostly-a-volatility)). The implication for the app is direct: every one of the seven strategies already carries an ATR-based stop, so sizing each call by inverse realised volatility (or to a portfolio volatility target) is a small change with the strongest evidence in this whole brief, and the SMA(50) timer should scale position size rather than block calls outright.

Two warnings temper this. The net-return effect of volatility scaling is sample-dependent (positive in 2022-2024 perpetuals, slightly negative in the 2017-2025 spot CAGR), so its unambiguous benefit is drawdown, not alpha, which is also what Cederburg and co-authors found when Moreira-Muir volatility management was re-estimated out of sample ([NBER w22208](https://www.nber.org/papers/22208)). And the temptation to add agreement filters (require Donchian, SMA(50) and two-week momentum to concur) has no peer-reviewed crypto support at daily bars; a 2025-26 study of lagged momentum and reversal rules on BTC and ETH found Sharpe differences "not statistically significant in paired bootstrap tests" and **Hansen's Superior Predictive Ability test did not reject the null after accounting for 300 cutoff-strategy combinations** ([JRFM 19(9):692](https://doi.org/10.3390/jrfm19090692)). Any AND-gate the app tries must be registered as a new strategy with its own random baseline and its variant count recorded in the multiple-testing denominator.

### A 28-indicator trend composite is the only peer-reviewed new entry, and its sample stops in May 2022

The one post-2022 publication that reports a long leg, a liquid-coin subset, turnover and break-even costs is Fieberg, Liedtke, Poddig, Walker and Zaremba's CTREND factor in the Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis. It aggregates 28 technical signals (moving averages, MACD, Bollinger bands, CCI, RSI, volume and volatility indicators) across horizons by machine learning on more than 3,000 coins, rebalances weekly and sorts into value-weighted quintiles over April 2015 to May 2022. Among the **100 largest coins the top quintile earned 3.78% a week gross and 3.38%, 3.24% or 3.11% net at 30/40/50 bps per trade, with 66.74% weekly turnover and a break-even cost of 2.83% per trade (1.56% at the 5% significance level)**; the authors state that "the CTREND effect originates mainly from the biggest and most liquid cryptocurrencies" ([Fieberg et al., JFQA 2025](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/trend-factor-for-the-cross-section-of-cryptocurrency-returns/4C1509ACBA33D5DCAF0AC24379148178)). This is new relative to Signal Lab's three-week cross-sectional Leaders strategy, which ranks on a single past return; CTREND is a multi-indicator ranking in which boll_mid, CCI and MACD carry the most weight, and it is the one place where machine learning in crypto has a defensible role, as a combiner of indicators in a weekly cross-sectional ranking rather than as a price forecaster.

The weaknesses are serious enough to make it a candidate to test rather than a result to adopt. In the second half of the sample (November 2018 to May 2022) the **long leg fell to 1.56% a week with t = 1.93, not significant alone**, and in bear-market weeks to 1.12% (t = 1.49); its alpha against the Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu market-size-momentum model is a marginal 1.10% a week (t = 1.94), so much of the long leg is market beta plus the bull-market component the same group warned about elsewhere ([Fieberg et al., JFQA 2025](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/trend-factor-for-the-cross-section-of-cryptocurrency-returns/4C1509ACBA33D5DCAF0AC24379148178)). The paper also carries a finding that bears on the app's existing horizons: moving from weekly to two-week rebalancing cuts the average weekly return by 1.5 points to 2.34%, returns stay significant to four-week holds, and **"most cryptocurrency momentum strategies no longer produce significant profits at a 3- or even 2-week horizon"**, which is mild support for a multi-indicator composite over single-signal momentum at the app's two- and three-week holds, with the caveat that all of it ends before the ETF era. The same research group's 2024 replication of crypto anomalies concludes that size and volume premia live in micro-caps, that momentum in larger coins "carries substantial trading costs, and its alphas come mostly from short positions", and that "most abnormal returns occur primarily in bull markets and fade over time" ([Fieberg, Liedtke and Zaremba, IRFA 2024](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finana/v94y2024ics1057521924001509.html)). Mercik, Zaremba and Demir's 2026 factor-zoo paper adds that **two to three factors, all liquidity variables, eliminate every significant portfolio alpha among 36 crypto factors** ([IRFA 113, 2026](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finana/v113y2026ics1057521926000645.html)), so any ranking the app adopts should be checked against a liquidity sort to confirm its long leg is not simply "illiquid coins".

### Low volatility helps on the long side; breakouts, seasonality, machine learning and arbitrage do not

A low-volatility or low-beta tilt within liquid coins has a favourable evidence pattern for a long-only app but no net figures. Three Finance Research Letters papers disagree by sample: no premium on 1,000 coins over 2013-2019, a "statistically and economically meaningful" premium after 2017 that is strongest with two- to three-month volatility formation and one-month holds, and in liquid coins **the long low-volatility leg earns the return while the short high-volatility leg adds nothing** ([FRL 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1544612326003818); [FRL 2021/22](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612321004116)). The catch is that BTC usually sits in the low-volatility bucket of any crypto cross-section, so a low-vol tilt within the top 50 largely reproduces a BTC/ETH overweight, and the 2026 paper finds the effect loses significance after controlling for beta, which points to a low-beta-to-BTC construction if the app tries it. On the breakout family the app already runs, Quantpedia's out-of-sample re-test of daily Gemini data (February 2022 to August 2024, no costs) found that a MAX rule buying x-day highs stayed effective with 20 days better than 10, while **a MIN rule buying x-day lows "performed poorly, with low or negative returns"** out of sample ([Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/revisiting-trend-following-and-mean-reversion-strategies-in-bitcoin/)); that is supportive of Donchian and near-7-day-high and a warning for the low-volume dip reversal, whose random-baseline record deserves a close look. Volatility-compression entries (Bollinger squeeze, open plus k times range, ATR expansion) have no academic test at all, and the only costed public backtest, a daily long-only BTC Bollinger breakout from 2022 to mid-2026 at 0.1% fee, **lost 14.2% with a 25.6% win rate and a 41.85% drawdown** ([CoinQuant](https://www.coinquant.ai/blog/volatility-trading-strategy-on-crypto-does-it-work-data-backed)).

Machine learning on OHLCV features has a mostly negative replication record. Baquero's 2026 systematic review of 23 Bitcoin-prediction studies finds directional accuracy "mostly in the low-to-mid 50s, peaking around 57%", naive models beating complex ones at 1-, 7- and 30-day horizons, OLS beating trees and neural nets cross-sectionally, and concludes that the field's "primary need is not more models but better evaluation" ([arXiv 2606.00071](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00071v1)). Where cross-sectional ML does show after-cost profits, Cakici and co-authors say the alphas "are concentrated in hard-to-trade assets and depend on extreme returns in small, illiquid, volatile coins" ([IRFA 2024](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finana/v94y2024ics1057521924001765.html)), and the main daily predictability an XGBoost finds on 3,703 coins is a **one-day reversal (out-of-sample R-squared 4.855%)** that cannot pay a 0.25 to 0.40% round trip ([RIBAF 2023](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0275531923000314)). Seasonality has moved into higher moments: Algieri and co-authors locate calendar anomalies "in the mean, variance, skewness, and kurtosis" without a trading-rule test ([NAJEF 2025](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/ecofin/v79y2025ics1062940825000816.html)), "Uptober" rests on **10 of 13 positive Octobers** with no significance test, October 2025 closed about -3.7%, and extending the window to year-end turns 7 of 9 positive years into 5 of 9 ([CoinJuice](https://coinjuice.com/research-hub/bitcoin-october-seasonality-13-years-of-data); [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/uptober-q4-returns-recalculated/)). The Fear and Greed index does not Granger-cause returns and gives no out-of-sample gain; returns lead sentiment ([ScienceDirect 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S305070062600006X)). Of twelve macro predictors in a real-time out-of-sample Bitcoin study, only the dollar index and the Shanghai composite produced trading profits, "and only during periods of large price swings", which is roughly what multiple testing predicts for two of twelve ([Arain and Snudden, Journal of Forecasting 2025](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/for.70077)). BTC dominance, the ETH/BTC ratio and the altcoin-season index have no tested evidence anywhere; they are lore.

Arbitrage and bots fail on structure rather than sample. A Finance Research Letters study of one week of Binance order-book data found 4,879 triangular opportunities on BTC/USDT, LTC/BTC and LTC/USDT, of which **96.93% were unprofitable even at the VIP-9 fee tier**; a regular-fee trader had 18 opportunities worth $12 to $18 for the whole week and would need to execute within 146 milliseconds on average ([Muck, Schmidl and Wolf, FRL 2025](https://fis.uni-bamberg.de/server/api/core/bitstreams/8b9ae900-017a-4bed-94b9-609c16e89945/content)). Chen, Chen and Jang prove that a static grid has **zero expected profit before fees** under a random price path and therefore negative expected profit after them; their dynamic variant beat BTC buy-and-hold over January 2021 to July 2024 "largely due to the significant rise in cryptocurrency prices" and did not beat it on ETH ([arXiv 2506.11921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11921)). Cointegration pairs trading against BTC earned a net Sharpe of about 0.97 on hourly Binance futures over 2021-2023 at a 0.04% taker fee, with costs already eating 12 to 48% of gross P&L and the 5-minute version "drastically negative"; it needs a short leg and hourly turnover, neither of which a spot app at 0.40% round trip can carry ([Tadi and Witzany, Financial Innovation 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40854-024-00702-7)).

## Derivatives data gives one permission and two vetoes, and carry has shrunk to 3%

The strongest long-side regime claim in the derivatives literature is also the easiest for Signal Lab to test, because the app already stores Binance funding since 2019. K33 reported in May 2026 that the 30-day average BTC funding rate had been negative for 67 consecutive days, the longest run this decade, and that **buying in negative-funding regimes since October 2018 had win rates of 83 to 96% over 30- to 360-day holds against 55 to 70% for random dates**, with average and median returns 1.84x to 6.27x random buying, lower average maximum drawdown and shorter time underwater ([The Block on K33](https://www.theblock.co/post/400182/bitcoin-longest-negative-funding-streak-this-decade-k33-flags-short-squeeze-risk)). VanEck independently found that since 2020 **30-day returns during negative funding averaged 11.5% against 4.5% across all periods and were positive 77% of the time**, that annualised funding below -5% was followed by 30-day returns averaging 19.4%, and that 19 of the top 50 180-day return windows began on negative-funding days although those are only about 13.6% of the sample ([VanEck via Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/vaneck-flags-dual-bullish-for-bitcoin)). The honest framing is that this is a multi-week, gross, small-sample result: K33's own September 2024 note counted the 30-day average turning negative "for just the seventh time since 2018" ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/315831/perp-signal-bitcoin-bottom-bullish-year-end-k33)), neither desk publishes methodology, neither conditions on price trend, and the only academic cross-sectional test of funding as a signal, on ten Binance perpetuals at an eight-hour horizon, "compels rejection of the hypothesis" that funding contains exploitable cross-sectional alpha ([SSRN 6701738](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6701738)). As a regime permission that loosens the SMA(50) gate or raises size for BTC and ETH when 30-day funding is negative, it is the one derivatives idea with two independent quantifications and a stated edge (about seven points over baseline at 30 days) that dwarfs the 0.25% round trip; it is new relative to the seven strategies and costs nothing in data.

Basis is the one derivatives variable with peer-reviewed predictive evidence, and it works as a veto. The BIS "Crypto carry" paper finds that Bitcoin and Ethereum carry **"averages above 10% annually, and can reach up to 60% per year"**, is driven by a convenience yield from smaller trend-chasing investors seeking leveraged upside against scarce arbitrage capital, and that **"high carry predicts future price crashes"**, with cash-and-carry itself risky because margins spike and positions are liquidated in drawdowns ([BIS WP 1087](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1087.pdf)). Chi, Hao, Hu and Ran find basis "the strongest signal predicting cross-sectional differences in cryptocurrency futures returns" on 2017-2021 data, with daily factor returns far stronger than weekly ([Journal of Futures Markets 2023](https://ideas.repec.org/a/wly/jfutmk/v43y2023i8p1161-1180.html)). For a long-only app the natural use is a size cut or entry veto when annualised quarterly basis is historically high; the threshold and horizon have to be estimated in-house because the readable BIS summary gives neither, and Binance serves annualised basis for perpetuals and quarterlies keyless but only 30 days deep ([Binance basis endpoint](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Taker-BuySell-Volume)). K33's second warning is the mirror image: when perpetual open interest jumped by more than 36,000 BTC in a week with rising funding during a drawdown, **six of seven similar regimes since 2020 saw continued declines over the following month with an average 30-day return of -16%** ([The Block on K33](https://www.theblock.co/post/379464/bitcoin-derivatives-market-forming-dangerous-setup-as-hopes-of-swift-bounce-fuel-rapid-leverage-climb-k33)); seven episodes from one firm is a veto on buying dips into leverage build-ups, not an entry. Deep backwardation as a contrarian long has only anecdote: CoinDesk noted the deepest CME backwardation since FTX in December 2025 while cautioning that it "does not automatically imply a bullish inflection" ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/12/03/bitcoin-futures-return-to-deepest-backwardation-since-ftx-collapse)).

The rest of the derivatives menu is either untested or tested and failed. "Buy the liquidation flush" has no supporting forward-return evidence anywhere; the two 2026 cascade papers find that taker order-flow variance compression is the only cross-event regularity and that it works "not as a per-event alarm" ([arXiv 2607.27070](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27070)), while VanEck's mid-2026 capitulation composite with 8 of 12 signals firing produced **mean 90- and 180-day returns of 12.8% and 32.0%, both below the unconditional 15.2% and 36.3%** ([VanEck via search](https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/blogs/digital-assets/matthew-sigel-vaneck-mid-august-2026-bitcoin-chaincheck/)). Liquidation totals are not available free in any case: Binance's stream has pushed only the largest liquidation per symbol per second since 2026-04-10, the public REST aggregate is gone and the signed `forceOrders` endpoints are limited to 90 days ([Binance change log](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/change-log)), and Coinglass starts at **$29 a month** with no free plan ([Coinglass pricing](https://coinglass.com/pricing)). OI-by-price quadrants and OI/market-cap thresholds have no forward-return test in 2023-2026; the only quantified claims are a closed TradingView script asserting "edge in backtesting" and a CryptoQuant contributor's unsourced 180-day moving average ([TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/script/D5187oQf-Open-Interest-Price-Quadrants-GBB/); [Cryptorank](https://cryptorank.io/news/feed/771d8-bitcoin-futures-flush-31-of-open-interest)). The CME weekend gap is dead twice over: across 427 weekends from December 2017 to May 2026 gaps filled within a week **80% of the time against 83% for a made-up level the same distance on the other side**, a fade lost more races than it won (161 to 231) before fees, and CME moved to near-24/7 trading on 29 May 2026 so no new gaps form ([Tradewize](https://tradewize.io/blog/how-to-read-the-cme-gap)). Binance's four positioning ratios (top-trader positions and accounts, global accounts, taker buy/sell) have no forward-return test at any horizon and daily commentary calls them "not a clean directional signal" ([Blockchain.News](https://blockchain.news/news/20261020-price-prediction-bnb-sell-side-pressure-caps-near-term)); the only peer-reviewed positioning evidence is weekly CFTC Commitments of Traders for CME Bitcoin, where leveraged funds "display market timing ability" mostly by adjusting shorts ([Baur and Smales, JFM 2022](https://ideas.repec.org/a/wly/jfutmk/v42y2022i7p1304-1323.html)), and since those funds are structurally the short leg of the basis trade their positioning largely mirrors the basis veto above. Options data supports DVOL as a regime variable (the variance risk premium is high and upside-tilted in the low-volatility regime per Almeida and co-authors, [arXiv 2410.15195](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15195)) but not as a directional signal: the only readable skew backtest finds **a +2.9% mean 30-day return after skew above +10 on 174 overlapping days with no baseline and no significance test** ([Tradewize](https://tradewize.io/blog/how-to-read-options-skew)), and max pain has nothing beyond anecdote, with Decrypt noting that $15 billion and $13.3 billion expiries produced muted reactions ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/376752)).

### Funding carry paid 11.9%, then 5.1%, then 3.0%, and October 2025 showed how hedges die

The yield side of derivatives is measurable from Binance's public funding history, and the measurement is sobering. Summing every settlement from `GET /fapi/v1/fundingRate` gives **BTC funding of 11.9% annualised in 2024, 5.1% in 2025 and 3.0% in 2026 to 21 October, with the share of negative-funding days rising from 6.6% to 8.2% to 23.8%**; ETH went 13.0%, 4.9%, 2.0%; and a simple average over the top-20 alts fell from **11.4% in 2024 to 1.3% in 2025 and -0.1% in 2026**, with BNB funding net negative on Binance every year from 2022 to 2025, so a hedged Launchpool farmer pays rather than earns ([Binance funding-rate endpoint](https://developers.binance.com/docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Rate-History); own computation in the research notes). The numbers cross-check: Ethena's risk page puts three-year BTC and ETH funding at 7.80% and 9.15% with 15.9% and 17.5% of days negative ([Ethena docs](https://docs.ethena.fi/solution-overview/risks/funding-risk)), and Coin Metrics puts 2024 at about 11% and 2025 at about 5% ([Ethena Q1 2026 report](https://stablecoininsider.org/ethena-usde-q1-2026-report/)). Against those gross figures stand a taker round trip of about 0.30% of notional on two legs, the entry and exit spot-perp spread, and the margin needed to survive a 50% rally on the short leg if the margin is USDT (roughly 1.5x notional of capital), which leaves **2025-2026 BTC carry on capital at about 2 to 3%**, below Aave v3 USDT's measured yearly means of 6.31% (2024), 4.00% (2025) and 2.99% (2026 to date) and Sky sUSDS at 4.75% and 3.72% ([DefiLlama yields API](https://yields.llama.fi/pools)). Quarterly cash-and-carry is no better: on 21 October 2026 Binance's December and March BTC contracts showed **5.07% and 5.18% annualised basis**, which nets to roughly 3.4 to 4.5% after fee drag, while the CME three-month basis sat near 3% against 3.8% on two-year Treasuries in August 2026 after running 15 to 20% in 2024 ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/binance-reclaimed-futures-dominance-cme-institutional-pullback/)).

The funding series also hides the two ways carry books were destroyed on 10 October 2025. After funding climbed from about 10% to nearly 30% annualised in the first week of October, more than **$19 billion was liquidated in about 24 hours, about 87% of it longs**, open interest fell 27.5%, and auto-deleveraging closed winning shorts across venues so that "some well-hedged shorts had their positions reduced or fully closed by auto-deleveraging, turning hedged books into naked exposure" ([FTI Consulting](https://www.fticonsulting.com/insights/articles/crypto-crash-october-2025-leverage-met-liquidity); [Galaxy Research](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/cryptos-flash-crash-liquidation-binance-adl-auto-deleveraging)). Simultaneously Binance's margin engine marked collateral at venue-local prints: **USDe traded to about $0.65, WBETH to about $430 and BNSOL to about $34.90** within minutes while USDe held near $1 elsewhere, and Binance paid about $283 million in compensation within a day ([21Shares](https://www.21shares.com/en-us/insights/why-did-ethenas-stablecoin-remain-stable-onchain-but-depegged-on-binance); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/344026/binance-reimburses-283m-after-market-crash-and-asset-depegging-issues)). Neither event appears in a ledger that records only funding received, which is why any carry sleeve in Signal Lab needs its own ledger design: the baseline is the account's own realised Simple Earn USDT rate read from `/sapi/v1/simple-earn/flexible/history/rateHistory` (Binance's public page renders only placeholders and the lone promotional anchor is "around a 1% Real-Time APR", [Binance Square](https://www.binance.com/de/square/post/349868913586274)), the excess is funding received minus funding paid, both legs' fees, the basis change and any collateral haircut over capital employed rather than notional, and the record must carry margin-ratio snapshots and ADL flags. The strategy that survives this arithmetic is not standing carry but *timed* carry, entering when trailing funding exceeds the stablecoin baseline plus a fee hurdle, which is exactly the kind of timing claim the app's random-entry baseline already tests. Options overwriting and liquidity provision do not belong in the sleeve at all: Anchorage's 37,000 Deribit backtests from October 2021 to April 2026 found an unfiltered **20-delta 30-day BTC covered call earned -0.5% net over the full period despite an 81% win rate**, and +5.2% a year only with regime and IV filters ([Anchorage](https://www.anchorage.com/insights/synthetic-yield-on-bitcoin-implementation-discipline-and-performance-boundaries-of-systematic-covered-call-writing)); Heimbach and co-authors found Uniswap v3 providers in volatile pools "on average lose money in comparison to holding the assets" and that "retail traders do not stand a chance" ([arXiv 2205.08904](https://arxiv.org/html/2205.08904v2)); and Binance's WBETH and BNSOL, yielding 2.45% and 5.08% in 2026 in token terms, are the very assets that printed 80 to 90% below their underlying on 10 October 2025 ([DefiLlama yields API](https://yields.llama.fi/pools); [Galaxy Research](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/cryptos-flash-crash-liquidation-binance-adl-auto-deleveraging)).

## On-chain feeds land three hours after the close and forecast volatility, not direction

A timing fact governs every daily on-chain signal before any statistics do. Coin Metrics' keyless community feed stamped its 20 October 2026 daily rows as available between **02:19 and 03:24 UTC on 21 October**, so a decision at the 00:00 UTC close of day D cannot use day D's on-chain row and must use D-1; bitcoin-data.com's free valuation metrics are additionally embargoed for seven days, and Blockchain.com's chart series lagged one to two days ([Coin Metrics API v4](https://docs.coinmetrics.io/api/v4); [BGeometrics free features](https://portal.bgeometrics.com/bguser/free-features.html)). Only exchange tickers, ETF flow tables published after the US close and DefiLlama's stablecoin totals are fresh enough to use on the same day's close. That built-in staleness matters because the on-chain evidence that exists is already at intraday speed.

Exchange netflows are the clearest case of a signal whose direction the literature cannot agree on and whose magnitude is unusable. Hoang and Baur's daily study of CryptoQuant reserves for 20 exchanges over 2016-2021 finds the change in exchange reserves "negative and statistically significant for all horizons" of future BTC returns, but publishes no coefficients, strategy or costs ([Quantpedia summary of SSRN 3902504](https://quantpedia.com/bitcoin-returns-and-volatility-predicted-by-bitcoin-exchange-reserves/)). The most recent study, Chi, Chu and Hao over December 2017 to January 2023, finds BTC net inflows "generally lack predictive power for BTC returns", with **adjusted R-squared of 0.001 to 0.002** and the one significant 4-hour coefficient carrying the opposite sign to Hoang and Baur; the positive result is USDT net inflows into exchanges predicting BTC and ETH returns at one- to two-hour horizons, and the only daily and weekly regressions reported are for volatility and insignificant ([arXiv 2411.06327](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06327)). The consistent finding is about volatility, which is a sizing input, and the free data cover only BTC and ETH: Coin Metrics serves `FlowInExNtv`, `FlowOutExNtv` and `SplyExNtv` keyless for those two from 2011 and 2015, returns no flow fields for XRP, DOGE, ADA, LTC or LINK, refuses `usdt_eth` exchange supply, and has no SOL or AVAX at all ([Coin Metrics API v4](https://docs.coinmetrics.io/api/v4)). Stablecoin supply fares no better as a lead: Kristoufek finds "no evidence of stablecoins boosting the prices of other cryptoassets" and that issuance "comes in reaction to the other cryptoassets price changes" ([FRL 2021](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v43y2021ics1544612321000726.html)), the positive results are either from the 2017 Tether era or intraday, aggregate USDT supply moved -0.3% day over day and -0.8% week over week in the probe, and no statistical test of the Stablecoin Supply Ratio exists; DefiLlama's keyless `stablecoincharts/all` nevertheless provides daily history from 26 November 2017 should the app want a slow 30-day supply-growth regime flag ([DefiLlama free API docs](https://api-docs.defillama.com/llms-free.txt)).

Valuation and cycle metrics have a usable asymmetry. A 2026 real-time audit of five top-calling thresholds against mechanically dated tops found that **Pi Cycle, MVRV above 3.7 and Mayer above 2.4 fired 210 to 240 days early for the November 2021 top and were silent at the October 2025 top**, while **MVRV below 1.0, Mayer below 0.8 and Puell below 0.5 all marked the November 2022 bottom within a week** and the 2015 and 2018 bottoms within four weeks; the mechanism is that per-epoch oscillator maxima shrink every cycle (MVRV 5.88, 4.72, 3.96, 2.74), and the 200-week moving average "failed in both directions" ([arXiv 2607.26188](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26188v1)). Shelton's out-of-sample test finds stock-to-flow and Metcalfe's law "have limited to no ability to predict Bitcoin's returns out-of-sample" ([JRFM 2024](https://ideas.repec.org/a/gam/jjrfmx/v17y2024i10p443-d1490211.html)). The defensible use in a long-only app is MVRV, Puell and Mayer as "cheap region" permissions, never as exit triggers, with the caveat that three or four prior bottoms dated after the fact is a handful of observations with inflated precision. Coin Metrics serves current MVRV (`CapMVRVCur`) free for BTC, ETH, XRP, DOGE, ADA, LTC and LINK, so a per-coin MVRV-below-1 permission is feasible for those seven and not for most of the Binance top 50 ([Coin Metrics API v4](https://docs.coinmetrics.io/api/v4)). Hash ribbons sit in the same bucket at lower confidence: Capriole counts nine historic buy signals with results "generally prepared with the benefit of hindsight" ([Capriole](https://capriole.com/hash-ribbons-bitcoin-bottoms)), an independent tally finds 14 signals with 64.29% profitable and no exit rule or fees ([Stopsaving](https://www.stopsaving.com/bitcoin-hash-ribbons-indicator/)), and the live 2025-26 episode began near $81,000 to $90,000 in late November 2025, lasted about three months as "one of the longest capitulations on record", and saw BTC fall to about $60,000 before any recovery cross ([CoinDesk, Nov 2025](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/11/27/hash-ribbon-flashes-signal-that-often-marks-cyclical-bottoms-for-bitcoin-price); [CoinDesk, Feb 2026](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/02/25/one-of-longest-mining-capitulations-nears-end-signaling-potential-btc-price-bottom)); with miners' debt rising from about $2.1 billion to $12.7 billion between mid-2024 and mid-2025 and power pivoting to AI, hash rate now responds to balance sheets as much as to price ([VanEck](https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/blogs/digital-assets/matthew-sigel-vaneck-mid-october-2025-bitcoin-chaincheck/)).

Spot ETF flows are the one on-chain-adjacent series fresh enough for a same-day close decision, and the best study says the published number is coincident, not predictive. Fabus and co-authors paired 386 sessions from 11 January 2024 to 28 July 2025 and found the naive same-day estimate of **4.89 percentage points per $1 billion shrinks to 1.67 once the return window starts after the ETF session, while "a window that starts after the flow is public shows no positive association"**; prior BTC returns robustly predict same-day flows under every timing convention ([SSRN 7545019](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7545019)). FalconX's earlier VAR found a small positive lagged-flow coefficient of 0.027 and an impulse response peaking at about 1.2% on days three to four, with no standard errors and an explicit warning about the ten-month sample ([FalconX](https://www.falconx.io/newsroom/what-can-spot-etf-flows-tell-us-about-the-trajectory-of-bitcoin-prices-a-preliminary-statistical-investigation)). Farside publishes the flows as an HTML table from 11 January 2024, fetchable with a browser User-Agent, but on the morning of 22 October 2026 the 21 October row was still partial, so the app should read the prior complete day ([Farside](https://farside.co.uk/bitcoin-etf-flow-all-data/)). Whale and smart-money tracking is tested and negative: Presto Labs' event study of 2021-2024 whale deposits to Binance for BTC, ETH and SOL produced **R-squared of 0.0017 to 0.0537** and the conclusion that deposits "lack sufficient predictive power to be a reliable trade signal" ([Presto Labs](https://www.prestolabs.io/research/whale-alerts-are-they-tradable)); the Philadelphia Fed finds whale alerts "induce a brief 24-hour spike in BTC volatility" and herd retail, with no return claim ([Philadelphia Fed WP 26-42](https://www.philadelphiafed.org/-/media/frbp/assets/working-papers/2026/wp26-42.pdf)); and the only cost-inclusive Hyperliquid copy-trading replay found **14 of 19 leaders lost money for the follower** at 5 bps slippage and 4.5 bps fee with a median skip rate of 91% ([hl-copy-core](https://github.com/winterchill-trading/hl-copy-core)). The Coinbase and Korea premiums are computable keyless at the UTC close from Coinbase, Upbit and ECB rates with history to 2016 and 2017, but no study regresses BTC returns on either; the kimchi-premium literature models the premium's own mean reversion and its lag structure with gold and KOSPI ([Economic Modelling 2024](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/ecmode/v135y2024ics0264999324000828.html)), and the one claim of a zero-crossing edge (+1.7% at seven days, +6.2% at 30) is journalistic and unverifiable ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/does-thes-kimchi-premium-still-front-run-btc/)). The academic network-value factor, a price-to-new-addresses sort whose cheapest quartile earned **1.5% a week gross on 127 coins over 2018-2021**, halved after 2020 and is not buildable free for the Binance top 50 because active-address data exists keyless for only seven coins; the same paper finds that new-address innovations "do not forecast cryptocurrency returns in the future", which contradicts "rising addresses, buy" directly ([Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu](https://economics.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2022-10/onchain_v18.pdf); [arXiv 2510.14435](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14435v4)).

## Listings, unlocks and delistings pay only as vetoes

The exchange-listing effect has been stable from 2019 to 2026 in shape, and the shape is hostile to a next-open buyer. Ante's event study of 327 listings found Binance's 45 events averaged **+14.7% on the listing day with 76% positive, then -6.3% over days +1 to +3 with only 24% positive**, and Upbit's 15 events showed a day +1 abnormal return of -2.9% with 20% positive ([Ante 2019](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Exploring-Market-Reactions-to-Exchange-Listings-of-Cryptocurrencies-BRL-working-paper3.pdf)). The Tie's 1,844 listing events from January 2023 to June 2026 across seven exchanges show BTC-adjusted returns rising before day 0, going flat or negative shortly after, and mean-reverting "within about two weeks", with no exchange statistically superior and the only positive-drift pocket, tokens already live on three or more major venues reaching a median of about +7% by day 9, resting on 14 observations ([The Tie 2026](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026); [The Tie, already-listed tokens](https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-a-binance-listing-delivers-for-already-listed-tokens)). Empirica's seven-year analysis of Binance listings puts the 2024 cohort at **+2.78% the day after the first close, +0.40% at one week, -22.66% at three months and -37.64% at six months, with 5.5% of listings positive at six months** ([Empirica](https://empirica.io/blog/the-binance-effect-a-7-year-analysis-for-token-founders/)). The capturable move is the pre-announcement run-up, which Wu Blockchain measured at a median +22.6% over the seven days before Coinbase announcements and +5.1% before Binance's ([Wu Blockchain](https://wublock.substack.com/p/investigation-reportis-there-insider)), and the first minutes after an Upbit notice, where case reports show +15% to +121% spikes that Presto calls "usually short-lived" ([Presto Labs](https://www.prestolabs.io/research/state-of-the-korean-crypto-market)). A daily or four-hour close enters after the spike into Ante's negative window; the right treatment of a second-venue listing for a coin the app already holds is a pump-candle veto and a possible exit prompt, pending the app's own event backtest.

Delistings are the asymmetric case where coding a veto costs nothing and the documented downside is enormous. No aggregate study exists, but the case record is uniform: WAVES fell nearly 30%, OMG 27% and XEM over 30% on Binance's June 2024 notice ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/298063/binance-to-delist-waves-omg-and-xem-on-june-17-sparking-price-plunges)), **TORN fell 57% in a day** in November 2023 ([Cointelegraph](https://regional-front.cointelegraph.com/news/tornado-cash-token-falls-binance-announces-delisting)), XMR dropped about 17% ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/215981/monero-binance-delists-xmr?amp=1)), and MARO closed -48% on Upbit's notice day ([Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/upbit-bloodbath-after-delisting-notices-shake-market/11586810)). Both exchanges publish ahead of the stop: "Binance Will Delist EPIC, QUICK on 2026-10-28" appeared on 14 October, and the keyless CMS catalog 161 carries spot delists, pair removals and Alpha removals in one feed ([Binance CMS catalog 161](https://www.binance.com/bapi/composite/v1/public/cms/article/list/query?type=1&pageNo=1&pageSize=5&catalogId=161)), while Upbit's keyless announcements API carries both the "거래지원 종료 안내" delisting notice and the earlier "거래 유의 종목 지정" caution stage ([Upbit announcements](https://api-manager.upbit.com/api/v1/announcements?os=web&page=1&per_page=20&category=trade)). The rule is "exit at the next open after a delisting notice for a held coin; block entries for coins on any delisting or caution list", with the parser distinguishing "Will Delist <coin>" from "Removal of Spot Trading Pairs".

Token unlocks are the best-studied event and the most consistent. Keyrock's analysis of more than 16,000 events found about **90% of unlocks create negative price pressure, with declines often starting about 30 days before and unlocks of 5 to 10% of supply producing drops roughly 2.4x more severe** ([Keyrock via CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/90-of-token-unlocks-drive-prices-down-declines-begin-a-month-ahead/)); a 2026 study of 52 Binance-listed unlocks found 46 negative within 72 hours with a mean of -16.97% ([SSRN 6632838](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6632838)); a 236-event benchmarked study puts the one-month post-unlock median at -16.26% against BTC with 72.5% of events lower; and Messari's 619 events show downside statistically significant only above 5% of circulating supply and absent below 2%, with weakness concentrated in the seven days around the event ([Streamflow compilation](https://streamflow.finance/blog/token-unlock-report)). Since **95.6% of priced unlock events are below one day's volume**, a veto window from T-14 to T+7 for unlocks above 2 to 5% of supply or above a day's volume fires rarely and mostly on the mid- and small-caps where the app's 0.40% tier applies. The data problem is real: DefiLlama's `/emissions` endpoint now returns HTTP 402 and lives only in the $300-a-month Pro API ([DefiLlama pricing](https://docs.llama.fi/pro-api)), and Tokenomist's API is paid with a request-only trial at 60 requests a minute and one year of history ([Tokenomist tiers](https://docs.tokenomist.ai/tier-overviews)), so the unlock veto is optional until a free feed appears.

The 2025 launch cohorts vindicate the 90-day new-coin exclusion and argue for lengthening it. Arrakis found **about 85% of 125 TGEs finished 2025 below their initial price, nearly two-thirds were already down within seven days, and only 9.4% of those recovered**, with up to 80% of airdrop recipients selling on day one ([The Defiant on Arrakis](https://thedefiant.io/news/research-and-opinion/only-1-in-10-weak-token-launches-recovered-in-2025-arrakis)); Delphi's 652 CEX-listed tokens since 2025 show a median return of -82% ([DigitalToday](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/84494/new-coins-since-2024-most-fail-to-hold-tge-price)); Upbit's 2025 KRW listings returned a median -80.9% from the day-one close to March 2026 and Binance's 2025 cohort -71.7% ([4Pillars](https://research.4pillars.io/en/research/2025-korean-cex-listings-post-mortem)). Empirica's Binance cohort drops another 15 points between months three and six, and The Tie notes small-cap underperformance "widens at two to three months", so a 180-day exclusion would avoid more losers at a measurable cost in universe size the ledger can quantify. Narrative or sector momentum has no backtest at all; yearly leaders flip (AI +2,939.8% and memes +2,185.1% in 2024, then memes -31.6% and AI -50.2% in 2025 while RWA gained 185.8%) and cap-weighted baskets diverge from medians (DePIN +17.0% cap-weighted against +1.2% median in May 2026), so a basket signal mostly reflects one or two large coins the app already sees ([Crowdfund Insider 2024](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2025/01/234794-ai-and-memecoins-were-most-profitable-crypto-narrative-in-2024-report/); [Crowdfund Insider 2025](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/?p=256921); [CryptoRank May 2026](https://cryptorank.io/insights/reports/narrative-overview-may-2026)). Memecoin survival statistics (pump.fun graduation below 1% from February 2025; 98.6% of surviving tokens classed as pump-and-dumps by Solidus Labs) support the pump-candle veto on low-rank coins rather than any momentum rule ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-memecoins-are-dying-at-record-rates-less-than-1-survive); [The Defiant on Solidus](https://thedefiant.io/news/defi/alarming-99-of-memecoin-launches-on-pumpfun-are-pump-and-dumps-or-rug-pulls-report)).

Copy trading, prediction markets, LLM sentiment and macro days complete the event list, none as an entry. The only multi-exchange copy-trading dataset found, vendor-published, has **97% of lead traders profitable but 48.48% of followers**, with MEXC followers net negative ([KuCoin blog on YieldFund](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/is-crypto-copytrading-profitable-in-2026)); Pelster and Hofmann found copied trades lose more in downturns ([Wikipedia summary, JBF 2018](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copy_trading)); and on Hyperliquid 166 of 1,000 analysed traders were profitable ([The Coin Republic](https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2025/06/16/hyperliquid-crypto-only-166-of-1000-traders-profitable-whats-going-on/)). Polymarket's 258 monthly BTC contracts over October 2024 to March 2026 show "no detectable predictive content for next-hour Bitcoin returns", and a one-touch barrier model explains over 95% of their prices from spot, strike, time and volatility, so prediction markets repackage spot and Deribit information rather than lead it ([Aalborg thesis](https://projekter.aau.dk/the-informational-content-of-prediction-markets-evidence-from-polymarket-bitcoin-contracts-628468cf.html)). The only crypto LLM-sentiment study with coefficients finds zero-shot GPT-4o sentiment from about 50 influencers' tweets predicts next-day BTC returns with **R-squared of 3.7% (8.2% with controls) in full-sample regressions, no out-of-sample test and no costs** ([Nguyen et al., CFRI 2025](https://www.emerald.com/cfri/article/doi/10.1108/CFRI-05-2024-0278/1301717/Decoding-market-sentiment-the-power-of-ChatGPT-in)), the equity analogue earns 34 bps a day gross and "declines as LLM adoption rises" ([Lopez-Lira and Tang](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.07619v6)), and X now charges $0.005 per post read, so a daily sweep of 2,000 posts costs more than a retail account's plausible edge ([postproxy](https://www.postproxy.dev/blog/x-api-pricing-2026/)). Scheduled macro days move volatility, not mean: across 62 CPI and 42 FOMC events from 2020 to 2025, **abnormal volatility of 0.81 and 1.03 percentage points of daily vol (p < 0.001) coincided with cumulative abnormal returns insignificant in every window and sub-group** ([KSE thesis](https://kse.ua/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/illia-nazaruk_268722_assignsubmission_file_nazaruk_final_thesis.pdf)), the New York Fed found Bitcoin "orthogonal to monetary and macroeconomic news" ([NY Fed SR 1052](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr1052)), and the renewed 2026 CPI sensitivity Block Scholes documents is "confined to the one-hour horizon" ([Block Scholes](https://www.blockscholes.com/institutional-research/is-bitcoin-showing-greater-sensitivity-to-us-cpi-releases-again)). The in-app analyst's defensible job is therefore risk: flag the calendar, widen stops or pause entries at the close before a release, never call direction.

Two consequences follow from the table. First, the only signals the app can backtest today with real history are Binance funding, Deribit DVOL, the spot-versus-perp volume ratio (computable from the spot and USDⓈ-M klines the app already pulls), the Coinbase and Korea premiums, stablecoin supply and the weekly CFTC file; everything served from `/futures/data/` and the Deribit options snapshot is archive-from-now, so a regime percentile on OI, basis or skew needs months of collection before any test is possible. Second, a once-a-day pull of every row in the table is trivial load (one weight-0 call per symbol per Binance endpoint, one Deribit book summary, one Hyperliquid POST, a handful of Coin Metrics requests spaced six seconds apart), well inside every published limit; the one feed that cannot be polled generously is bitcoin-data.com at 15 requests a day. Glassnode's API is Professional-plan only, CryptoQuant's free Basic plan excludes on-chain data and keeps 30 days of history, and Dune's free tier is view-only, so none of the vendor dashboards that generate the lore are reachable from a keyless phone ([Glassnode FAQ](https://docs.glassnode.com/further-information/faq); [CryptoQuant API plans](https://cryptoquant.com/apis); [Dune billing](https://docs.dune.com/api-reference/overview/billing)).

## Going live is an Ed25519 key, an intent log and a legal question

The Binance Spot API a phone would talk to in October 2026 differs from the 2023 version in four ways that matter. `POST /api/v3/order` is unchanged in shape, with `MARKET` orders accepting `quoteOrderQty` to size in USDT, `STOP_LOSS_LIMIT` for a resting safety stop, and `LIMIT_MAKER` for a fee-saving entry that is rejected rather than filled if it would take ([Trading endpoints](https://developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/rest-api/trading-endpoints)). Since about 2 April 2026 **successful order placements cost zero request weight**, the listenKey user-data stream was **removed on 20 February 2026** in favour of `userDataStream.subscribe.signature` over the WebSocket API (which works with any key type), OCO lives at `POST /api/v3/orderList/oco` with the old path marked deprecated, and since 15 January 2026 **signed payloads must be percent-encoded before signing or the request fails with -1022** ([Changelog](https://developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/CHANGELOG)). Binance states that "it is highly recommended to use Ed25519 API keys"; HMAC-SHA256 still works on REST, `recvWindow` defaults to 5,000 ms with a 60,000 ms maximum, and the timing rule rejects any timestamp more than one second ahead of server time ([General API information](https://developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/rest-api/general-api-information)). Idempotency rests on `newClientOrderId`, which must be "unique among open orders"; a duplicate while the first order is open is rejected with -2010 "Duplicate order sent.", and after a -1007 timeout ("execution status unknown") the client must query `GET /api/v3/order` by `origClientOrderId` rather than resend ([Errors](https://developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/errors)). The pattern that falls out is a Room-persisted intent (symbol, side, quantity, deterministic client ID such as `sl2-<strategy>-<symbol>-<candleOpenMs>-<side>`, state SENDING) written before the HTTP call, a query-then-resolve step on any timeout, and a blind-retry ban, because a retry after a *filled* order would be accepted. The daily reconciliation is `GET /api/v3/account?omitZeroBalances=true` (weight 20) plus `openOrders` per tracked symbol (weight 6), with any unexplained balance or missing stop putting that symbol into a "needs attention" state that blocks new entries. Live `exchangeInfo` on 21 October 2026 gives **6,000 request weight per minute, 100 unfilled orders per 10 seconds, a 5 USDT minimum notional applied to market orders, a BTC step of 0.00001, and five algo orders per symbol**, so the real constraints are fee drag and the five-stop cap if several strategies stack on one coin, not exchange floors ([Filters](https://developers.binance.com/docs/binance-spot-api-docs/filters)). Quantities must round *down* to `stepSize` in BigDecimal, prices to `tickSize`, with a few percent of margin on the notional check because the engine uses its own five-minute average or reference price.

Key permissions are simpler than the folklore. The 2021 rule that unrestricted-IP keys lost "Enable Spot & Margin Trading" after 90 days was **withdrawn as of 24 October 2023** ([Binance announcement](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/updates-to-api-key-permission-rules-2021-07-26-11e4c2f44e7a47b9b5fc0e479c0b256f)). Today an unrestricted-IP HMAC key "won't have any permission other than reading", withdrawals always require an IPv4 whitelist, Binance names self-generated Ed25519 or RSA keys as the alternative to IP restriction, and a key is created only on a KYC-verified account with 2FA ([How to Create API Keys](https://binance.com/en/support/faq/how-to-create-api-360002502072)); an unrestricted key inactive for 30 days is deleted ([How to Use API Keys Safely](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/276340)). A phone on mobile data has no stable IPv4, so Ed25519 is the natural Android choice and it rules out withdrawals by construction; regular users get up to five sub-accounts, so the live key can be scoped to a sub-account whose balance is the loss limit ([Binance Sub-Account FAQ](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/binance-sub-account-functions-and-frequently-asked-questions-360020632811)). Fees for a Regular User are **0.10% maker and taker, 0.075% with BNB**, with USDC pairs at 0.095% taker ([Binance Fee Schedule](https://www.binance.com/en/fee/schedule)); the app's 0.25% and 0.40% round-trip assumptions therefore leave about 0.05 to 0.25% of implicit slippage budget, which the Binance BTC-USDT book easily honours ($16 million bid and $26 million ask within 1% of mid on 1 December 2025, per [Kaiko](https://resources.kaiko.com/hubfs/Liquidity%20Flywheel%20Drives%20Binance%20to%20300%20Million%20Users.pdf)) but which a $10,000 order in a rank-35 alt would strain. The worst minute to send a market order is the one the paper ledger assumes: 00:00 UTC is simultaneously the daily candle boundary and a Binance funding timestamp, and intraday data show "a large burst in volatility during the first minute of the hour", strongest at the four-hourly marks ([arXiv 2109.12142](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12142)); a live run that enters at 00:03 should record the fill and the official open separately so the realised gap is measured. The free Spot testnet (GitHub login, same `/api` surface and filters, virtual balances, "reset to a blank state roughly once a month, without notice", no `/sapi`) is adequate for exercising signing, filters and crash reconciliation and useless for fills; `POST /api/v3/order/test` with `computeCommissionRates=true` against production is the zero-risk dry run on real prices ([Spot Testnet](https://testnet.binance.vision/); [rest-api.md](https://github.com/binance/binance-spot-api-docs/blob/master/rest-api.md)).

On the Android side the secret should live wrapped by a hardware-backed Keystore AES key gated by `BIOMETRIC_STRONG`, with the blob in `getNoBackupFilesDir()` and excluded from both `<cloud-backup>` and `<device-transfer>`; StrongBox supports RSA, AES, P-256 and HMAC but not Ed25519, so the Ed25519 seed is unwrapped into process memory only for the run ([Android Keystore](https://developer.android.com/privacy-and-security/keystore); [Auto Backup](https://developer.android.com/identity/data/autobackup)). Jetpack's `EncryptedSharedPreferences` was **deprecated in April 2025** in favour of direct Keystore use ([Security release notes](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/security)). Auth-per-use and an unattended 00:02 run are in tension; the two workable designs are an "armed window" in which the owner authenticates in the evening for the next run only, or "notify-then-confirm" in which the alarm computes the orders and the owner approves with biometrics before anything is sent, losing at-the-open timing but keeping per-order consent. The existing `setExactAndAllowWhileIdle` alarm survives Doze (once per nine minutes per app suffices), `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` is user-revocable so each run must check `canScheduleExactAlarms()` and degrade to paper, and a WorkManager fallback should reconcile if the alarm path failed ([Doze](https://developer.android.com/training/monitoring-device-state/doze-standby); [Schedule alarms](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/alarms/schedule)). Binance has no account-level daily-loss limit, so the kill switch is app-side: a global live flag checked before every order, per-day loss and order-count caps, per-symbol notional caps, and auto-disable on any balance mismatch, on -2015, on a 418 ban, or when `canTrade` is false.

The legal question outranks the technical one for an owner in Egypt. The Central Bank of Egypt restated in September 2022 that **Law 194 of 2020 "prohibits issuing, trading, or promoting cryptocurrencies, creating or operating platforms for trading it"** without a CBE licence, with fines of EGP 1 to 10 million and/or prison, and 2026 secondary guides report that "no licenses have ever been issued" and that Egyptian banks block transfers to international exchanges ([Mariblock on the CBE statement](https://www.mariblock.com/central-bank-of-egypt-send-a-reminder-about-cryptocurrency-ban); [MEXC blog](https://blog.mexc.com/news/is-crypto-legal-in-egypt-2026-complete-law-guide/)). Egypt does not appear on any third-party list of Binance's prohibited countries and Binance runs EGP P2P markets with Egyptian bank rails ([Binance P2P](https://p2p.binance.com/en/trade/NBE/USDT?fiat=EGP)), so the exposure is not Binance's list but the law's wording, which names "trading" itself; this is what the sources say, not legal advice, and a local lawyer is the right next step. The contrast elsewhere is sharp: Binance FZE has held a full VARA licence in Dubai covering retail investors since **15 April 2024** ([VARA register](https://www.vara.ae/en/licenses-and-register/public-register/binance-fze/)), Turkey has licensed providers under Law 7518 since 2 July 2024 ([Mondaq](https://mondaq.com/turkey/fin-tech/1498498/the-law-no-7518-amending-the-capital-markets-law-has-entered-into-force-what-now)), Saudi Arabia has no licensed exchange and no explicit ban ([Library of Congress](https://blogs.loc.gov/law/2025/01/falqs-regulation-of-cryptocurrencies-in-the-gulf-cooperation-council-countries-part-one/)), and Binance's withdrawal from several EU states on **1 July 2026** after abandoning its MiCA application shows access can vanish with days of notice ([Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/06/25/binance-to-halt-crypto-services-across-eu-countries-after-failing-to-secure-mica-approval)). No jurisdiction surveyed requires an individual to hold a licence to automate orders on their own account; licensing attaches to service providers. The residual risks are Binance's stated discretion to "restrict or terminate a Binance API connection at any time for any reason" ([Convert API Introduction](https://developers.binance.com/docs/convert/Introduction)), geographic withdrawal, and bank-rail blocking, which argues for a small place/cancel/query/account interface from day one so the data and order layers can move to Bybit's `GET /v5/market/kline` with the same `BTCUSDT` symbol convention if needed ([Bybit V5 Kline](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/market/kline)).

Realism closes the loop. The BIS found that "a majority of crypto app users in nearly all economies made losses on their bitcoin holdings" from 2015 to 2022, with large holders selling and retail buying around Terra and FTX ([BIS Bulletin 69](https://www.bis.org/publ/bisbull69.htm)); eToro account data show the same traders who are contrarian in stocks "follow a momentum-like strategy in cryptocurrencies" ([Kogan et al., NBER w31317](https://www.nber.org/papers/w31317)). The arithmetic of a real edge is modest: at two to four trades a week and 25% of the account per position, **a 0.5% net edge per trade is worth $130 to $260 a year on $1,000 and $2,600 to $5,200 on $20,000; a 1% edge doubles that**, and a 0.5% net edge on alts requires roughly 0.9% gross at the app's 0.40% cost assumption. With a per-trade return standard deviation near 10%, detecting a 0.5% edge at t = 2 needs about **1,600 trades, and a 1% edge about 400**, which at the app's frequency is two to fifteen years, so the ledger's "beats random entries" verdict on any single strategy is weak evidence and pooling strategies and trading more coins per signal (more trades, not bigger ones) is what shortens the wait. Sizing dominates everything else: under geometric Brownian motion a full-Kelly position faces a **50% chance of a 50% drawdown, half Kelly cuts that to 12.5% while keeping 75% of the growth** ([Geboers and Depaire, Entropy 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9955835)), the Kelly-consistent band for e = 0.5 to 0.75% and sigma = 10 to 12% is 15 to 40% of the account per position, and overestimating the edge by two, the usual backtest optimism, turns half Kelly into full Kelly. Four 25% positions in correlated alts behave like one 100% position when BTC moves, so the cap belongs on total exposure, and the owner should pre-register the drawdown that would count as failure, for instance the 5th percentile of the random-baseline drawdown distribution.

## Conclusion

The pattern across six independent sweeps is that the crypto edge industry sells entries while the evidence pays for exits, sizes and vetoes. Every candidate in the top half of the ranking either changes how much the app buys (volatility scaling, gate-as-size, low-beta tilt), when it is allowed to buy (negative funding, cheap-region metrics) or when it must not (delistings, unlocks, new coins, high basis, leverage build-ups, macro days), and all of them attach to the seven strategies Signal Lab already runs without adding to the multiple-testing denominator the way a new entry rule does. The single new entry with peer-reviewed support, the multi-indicator composite, is best read as a redesign of the Leaders strategy to be tested against its random baseline with its variant count recorded, not as a proven source of 3% a week. For an app whose stated purpose is an honest ledger, that reordering is itself the finding: the next milestones are worth more as sizing and veto machinery than as a longer strategy list.

Two implications reach beyond strategy selection. The arithmetic of 400 to 1,600 trades means the ledger cannot, on its own, promote any single strategy to real money within a plausible horizon; pooling evidence across strategies and coins, pre-registering a failure drawdown, and treating the random-baseline comparison as a filter against gross mistakes rather than a proof of edge is the realistic stance. And the move to live orders is, in October 2026, a well-understood engineering job with a clear recipe (Ed25519 key wrapped in Keystore, persisted intents, query-before-retry, reconciliation every run, an app-side kill switch, orders sent a few minutes after the burst at 00:00 UTC) whose gating question is not technical at all but whether trading is lawful where the owner lives, and whether a venue that left the EU with a week's notice is one to build a position-management loop on without a second-venue abstraction from the first commit.
